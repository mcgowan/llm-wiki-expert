# Overview

In this episode, journalist Karen Hao — author of *Empire of AI: Dreams and Nightmares in Sam Altman's OpenAI* — tells Steven Bartlett that the leading AI companies function like colonial empires: laying claim to data and intellectual property that is not theirs, exploiting hundreds of thousands of contract workers worldwide, monopolizing knowledge production, and justifying it all with a narrative that they are the "good empire" racing a bad one (usually China, and in OpenAI's early days, Google).[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk] Hao says her account draws on more than 300 interviews with over 250 people, including more than 90 current or former OpenAI employees and executives, along with internal documents and correspondence.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]

A central thread of Hao's argument is that "artificial general intelligence" has no coherent scientific definition, which lets leaders like Sam Altman redefine it opportunistically for different audiences — a cancer-curing savior for Congress, a digital assistant for consumers, a system generating a hundred billion dollars of revenue in the Microsoft deal.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk] She contends that both the utopian and doomer narratives are two sides of one myth that AI executives actively cultivate — and partly come to believe themselves — because it persuades the public, investors, and governments to cede them power, money, and control.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]

Hao recounts the inside story of Altman's 2023 board firing and reinstatement, the pattern of co-founders and executives leaving to build rival labs, and the human and environmental costs of the scaling race — from laid-off professionals absorbed into punishing data-annotation work to communities in Memphis and Texas living beside gas-turbine-powered supercomputer facilities.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk] Her prescription is not to abandon AI but to "break up the empires": regulate the industry, withhold data, contest data centers, and build efficient, targeted "bicycles of AI" like AlphaFold instead of resource-devouring "rockets".[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]

# Key points

- Hao says the term "artificial intelligence" was coined by John McCarthy in 1956 despite there being no scientific consensus on what human intelligence is, which she argues leaves the field — and the term AGI — with no fixed goalposts that companies can be held to.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- According to Hao, Altman has defined AGI differently for different audiences: curing cancer and poverty when addressing Congress, an amazing digital assistant when selling to consumers, a system generating $100 billion in revenue in the Microsoft deal, and "highly autonomous systems that outperform humans at most economically valuable work" on OpenAI's website.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Hao argues Altman's famous 2015 existential-risk blog post mirrored Elon Musk's rhetoric to recruit him as an OpenAI co-founder, and that her reporting shows Altman later persuaded Greg Brockman (who persuaded Ilya Sutskever) to make him, not Musk, CEO of the for-profit entity — prompting Musk's exit and lasting vendetta.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- She reports that people who know Altman are extremely polarized — seeing him either as this generation's Steve Jobs or as manipulative and dishonest — and that the split tracks whether they share his vision of the future; Dario Amodei left to found Anthropic after feeling his skills had been used for a vision he opposed.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Drawing on roughly six or seven sources, Hao recounts that Sutskever and CTO Mira Murati brought documented concerns about Altman's behavior to OpenAI's independent board members — including the discovery that the OpenAI Startup Fund was actually Altman's fund — leading to his abrupt firing, the backlash, and his reinstatement days later; Sutskever and Murati subsequently left.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Hao observes that nearly every tech billionaire now has their own AI company — Musk's xAI, Amodei's Anthropic, Sutskever's Safe Superintelligence, Murati's Thinking Machines Lab — because each wants to create AI in their own image after clashing with Altman.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- She likens the industry's mythmaking to the Messiah myth in *Dune*: executives like Amodei (who has put the odds of civilizational catastrophe at 10-25%) knowingly craft the myth yet also lose themselves in it.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Hao compares industry-funded AI research to climate science bankrolled by fossil fuel companies, citing Google's firing of ethical-AI co-leads Timnit Gebru and Margaret Mitchell over a critical paper, and OpenAI's subpoenas of nonprofit critics during its for-profit conversion.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- On jobs, Hao argues losses come both from real model capabilities and from executives choosing "good enough" automation; she cites a New York Magazine piece showing laid-off professionals — even award-winning directors — ending up in inhumane data-annotation work, training models on the very jobs they lost, which breaks the career ladder by gutting entry- and mid-level roles.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- In a live phone call during the episode, Klarna CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski says AI handles most customer-service volume, that headcount has fallen from about 6,000 to under 3,000 through natural attrition while revenue doubled, and that the future of customer service is human "VIP" service.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Hao details environmental harms: OpenAI's Stargate data center in Abilene, Texas would run a million chips on more than a gigawatt of power, a Meta facility in Louisiana would be four times larger, and Musk's Colossus in Memphis used 35 methane gas turbines that residents of a working-class Black and brown community discovered by smelling gas in their homes.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
- Her proposed alternative is "bicycles of AI" — small, curated-data systems like DeepMind's Nobel Prize-winning AlphaFold — plus collective pressure: she notes 80% of Americans in a recent poll want the AI industry regulated, and cites data-center protests and IP lawsuits already stalling the empires' plans.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]

# Takeaways

Hao's closing message is that the question of whether individual AI CEOs are good or bad people matters less than the governance structure that lets a handful of companies make decisions affecting billions who get no say; she urges listeners to see themselves as data donors and community stakeholders with leverage — to withhold data, contest data centers, shape AI-adoption policies at work and school, and support alternatives — so that AI's genuine utility can be preserved without the extractive imperial model that currently produces it.[^ai-whistleblower-we-are-being-gaslit-by-ai-compa-Cn8HBj8QAbk]
