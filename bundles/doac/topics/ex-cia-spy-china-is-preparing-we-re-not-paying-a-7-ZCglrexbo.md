# Overview

Steven Bartlett interviews Mike Baker, who joined the CIA's operations directorate at 22 in the early 1980s, spent his entire agency career overseas without a headquarters tour, and later co-founded a strategic intelligence and investigations firm doing risk, fraud and market work for corporate clients.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] Baker opens with tradecraft, describing the recruitment cycle — spotting, targeting, development, recruitment, running — and arguing that persuading a foreign official to betray country, service and family is the hardest sale there is, which is why the same skills of profiling, mirroring and identifying leverage transfer so cleanly into business development.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] He distils the rest of his agency training into what he calls company rules: define and communicate the mission, know your risk appetite, know your operating environment, and above all "get off the X" — make a decision with imperfect information rather than be paralysed on the ambush site.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]

The bulk of the conversation is a map-in-hand tour of what Baker sees as a world of problems rather than a community of nations, recorded weeks after Donald Trump's 2024 election win.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] On Ukraine he is deliberately unsentimental: he argues total Ukrainian victory is not achievable, that continued Western support is the precondition for a negotiated settlement rather than an obstacle to peace, and that the war ends in 2025 with lines roughly near where they are and some form of buffer zone.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] On China — his answer to Bartlett's question about the West's real adversary — he relays the view of experts he trusts that Beijing already behaves as though it is at war with the United States while the West treats it as a mere economic competitor.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]

Baker's central warning is about the shape of the next big conflict rather than its trigger. He says critical infrastructure in the US is being probed and mapped every day so that an adversary has a playbook to switch off power, water treatment, fuel, pharmaceuticals, food distribution and cash access, bringing the pain directly to the homeland, with a parallel campaign against satellites and GPS.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] Alongside that sits what he considers the live, ongoing front: disinformation campaigns from China, Russia and Iran that exploit Western social divisions the same way an intelligence officer exploits a recruit's weaknesses, with the individual smartphone user as the only real line of defence.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]

# Key points

- Baker describes the asset recruitment cycle — spotting, targeting, development, recruitment, running — and stresses the vocabulary correction that the American is the CIA officer while the recruited foreign source is the agent or asset.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On motivation, he says ideology is rarely the lever: money is the big one, alongside a sick child who cannot get treatment, feeling disrespected by one's own government, or personal grievance — and he cites FBI mole Robert Hanssen, who volunteered his services and structured the relationship so the Russians never learned his identity, betraying US assets who "didn't have a happy ending."[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- He argues an asset relationship has a closing window from the moment of recruitment because betrayal is corrosive to a normal person's character, and that CIA training runs a couple of years covering tradecraft, clandestine communications, disguise, surveillance and counter-surveillance, plus paramilitary work.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- His business lesson is "getting off the X" — the X being the ambush site: any major decision reduces to two or three real options, listing pros and cons mostly breeds paralysis, and the inertia of not acting carries its own cost.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On Ukraine he cites a Kyiv institute's tracking poll in which willingness to fight indefinitely fell from 73% of citizens at the start of 2024 to 63% by the previous month, alongside a roughly 3:1 Russian manpower advantage, a front line of about 600-620 miles, destroyed energy infrastructure heading into winter, and only one night in September and October 2024 when Kyiv was not under drone attack.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- He says Russia would hold about 20% of Ukraine if fighting stopped immediately, that Ukraine's Kursk incursion put foreign troops on Russian soil for the first time since World War II but overstretched its supply lines, and that Putin has massed roughly 40,000 Russian and 10,000 North Korean troops to retake it — troops he expects to be used quickly and treats as expendable in what he calls a meat-grinder operation.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- Baker's read on the Kim-Putin deal is that North Korea gains money, combat experience for a military that has none, and weapons technology transfer, while Putin gains bodies that let him avoid another deeply unpopular conscription round.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On Taiwan he says the 2049 reunification timeline has shrunk to something Xi Jinping intends to complete within his own tenure, points to the largest-ever Chinese military exercise encircling the island and to Hong Kong's dismantled democracy as a template for a softer takeover, and predicts that no US president will actually put boots on the ground regardless of what they say publicly.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- He names Iran as the pressing short-term threat: assassination plots against the president-elect, Mike Pompeo, Mark Milley, Mark Esper and Brian Hook in retaliation for the 2020 killing of Qassem Soleimani, plus a proxy network — Hezbollah, Hamas, the Houthis, Islamic Jihad — built around the stated goal of destroying Israel, which makes ceasefires "lipstick on a pig" or "a Band-Aid on a sucking chest wound."[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On October 7 he argues Hamas, with IRGC awareness and training, deliberately lulled Israel to sleep by pretending to care only about economic development, and defeated signals intelligence by reverting to handwritten notes passed between relatives — the same low-tech method al-Qaeda adopted once it understood US intercept capability.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On nuclear risk he says talking about limited use normalises it, recalls the Soviet Dead Hand system that removed humans from retaliation decisions and warns against letting AI do the same, and predicts that an Iranian bomb would trigger Saudi Arabia to follow and cascade proliferation across the region.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- On TikTok he stops short of the data-harvesting claim but agrees with Bartlett's framing that a hostile state could simply build a maximally addictive algorithm and let it sow division, noting pointedly that Chinese citizens cannot access TikTok inside China — and he adds that Beijing collects mass information indiscriminately anyway, not just on obvious targets.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
- His preparedness advice is modest rather than survivalist: read widely across outlets you disagree with, notice signals early (he says he was getting notifications about quarantined Chinese cities and the Wuhan lab well before any official declaration), and have an ordinary plan for a natural disaster or blackout.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]

# Takeaways

Baker's framing throughout is that Western complacency is itself the vulnerability: nations that have not experienced war on their own soil in living memory mistake the absence of visible shooting for peace, while adversaries run infrastructure mapping and influence operations that already constitute conflict by another name.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] His prescription is unglamorous — take hostile leaders literally at their word rather than mirroring Western values onto them, keep enough support flowing to force adversaries to a negotiating table, and pause before resharing anything to ask where the information came from, since he considers the curious individual the only real defence against disinformation.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo] Asked what made him successful, he credits his father's example of hard work and fierce loyalty over any professional skill, and says the advice he gives his own children is simply to work a little harder than everyone else because it is the one variable they control.[^ex-cia-spy-china-is-preparing-we-re-not-paying-a-7-ZCglrexbo]
