# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews Dr David Unwin, a UK general practitioner named in 2018 among the ten most influential doctors in the United Kingdom, who argues that the world is "sleepwalking into a metabolic disaster" driven by hidden dietary sugar — above all the starchy carbohydrates most people believe are healthy.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s] Unwin explains that when people eat more carbohydrate than they burn, insulin converts the excess glucose to fat that accumulates in the belly, liver, and eventually the pancreas — a "long silent scream from the liver" (a phrase he attributes to Professor Roy Taylor of Newcastle University) that can run for a decade before it collapses insulin production and tips into type 2 diabetes.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]

Unwin recounts how a furious patient in 2012 — who normalized her blood sugar without metformin after learning online that "bread is sugar" — upended his 25 years of guideline-driven practice and led him and his psychologist wife Jen to run free Monday-night low-carb groups with volunteer patients, producing improvements in liver function, weight, blood pressure, and HbA1c that he says he had never seen in a quarter-century of prescribing.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s] He presents his "teaspoon of sugar equivalents" system, built on glycemic load, to show that everyday staples such as boiled rice, baked potatoes, and cornflakes affect blood glucose more than a chocolate bar.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]

The conversation also covers ultra-processed food addiction (which Jen Unwin's work suggests affects roughly 14% of the population), behavior change via her "GRIN" model, the diabetes-cancer link, continuous glucose monitors as feedback tools, the string test for waist-to-height ratio, and magnesium deficiency in the modern food supply.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]

# Key points

- Unwin says that when he started practice in 1986 his surgery had no cases of type 2 diabetes under age 55 — the disease was then called "maturity-onset diabetes" — whereas he now sees poorly controlled diabetes in patients under 25, and estimates a third of people worldwide with type 2 diabetes do not know they have it.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- Citing UK government figures, he claims every year of poorly controlled type 2 diabetes costs about 100 days of life expectancy, and notes cancer (eight forms strongly associated with diabetes) is a rising cause of death in diabetics alongside cardiovascular damage.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- He describes the disease pathway as excess carbohydrate → insulin stores glucose as fat in belly and liver → fatty liver (now affecting about a third of the developed world) → insulin resistance → pancreatic fat → collapse of insulin production, referencing Professor Roy Taylor's "long silent scream from the liver" over roughly 10 years.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- From his own practice dataset kept since 2013, he reports 93% of pre-diabetic patients achieve normal blood sugar on a low-carb diet, falling to about 73% if treated early in type 2 diabetes and roughly 50% after five more years — "a stitch in time" — and says he has achieved drug-free remission 157 times.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- Using his teaspoon-of-sugar-equivalent system (glycemic load restated in 4 g teaspoons), he shows 150 g of boiled rice equals about 10 teaspoons of sugar, a baked potato 9, a bowl of unfrosted cornflakes 8, a chocolate bar 7.5, and a ripe banana 6 — shocking the host, who assumed rice and potatoes contained almost none.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- He explains starch as "glucose molecules holding hands" that digestion frees back into sugar, which is why unsweetened bread, rice, and cereal raise blood glucose; a whole normal blood supply contains only about one sugar cube's worth of glucose, so small dietary loads overwhelm regulation in the insulin resistant.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- Unwin admits he spent 25 years blaming patients for failed "eat less, move more" advice, was effectively incentivized through practice payments to keep patients on metformin, and calls his conversion — sparked by the patient "Mrs Jones" and John Briffa's book Escape the Diet Trap — a source of shame and epiphany.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- His infographics, distributed free in 35 languages through the Public Health Collaboration charity he co-founded with Dr Rangan Chatterjee, target consumer confusion over foods marketed with a health halo — dried fruit snacks at 60-70% sugar, white chocolate around 70-80% sugar, barbecue sauce with about 30 sugar cubes per bottle, and fruit juices and smoothies.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- He and his wife Jen argue ultra-processed food addiction is real — affecting about 14% of the population per her published work — illustrated by a wealthy patient who ate bleach-sprayed bread from the bin at 4 a.m.; that patient reached surgery-ready blood sugar only through combined low-carb diet, a continuous glucose monitor, and a low-dose GLP-1 drug.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- Jen Unwin's "GRIN" model distills CBT into four steps — Goals, Resources, Increments, and Noticing — which Unwin demonstrates live on Bartlett as his standard consultation tool for motivating behavior change rather than dispensing advice.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- Simple self-checks he recommends include the string test (waist should be less than half your height) and cheap continuous glucose monitors; he warns the UK's healthy life expectancy has fallen about two years in a decade, that ultra-processed food costs English taxpayers an estimated 7,000 pounds each per year, and that the US has the world's largest healthspan-to-lifespan gap.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
- He also argues modern depleted soils make adequate dietary magnesium nearly impossible, recommending supplementation (citrate for constipation, glycinate or threonate for sleep and mood) — illustrated by his story of a farmer who lost 15 cows to magnesium-deficiency "staggers."[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]

# Takeaways

Unwin's core message is that metabolic disease is largely a consequence of everyday carbohydrate choices people do not recognize as sugar, and that it is substantially preventable and often reversible — the earlier the intervention, the higher the odds.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s] He urges listeners to treat themselves as an experiment: pick a specific health future, base meals on protein and green vegetables, learn the true glycemic load of foods, measure results (blood tests, waist string test, or a continuous glucose monitor), and approach food addiction with honesty and gentle support rather than blame.[^fatty-liver-expert-the-toxic-ingredient-silently-zc8Nh4TMB1s]
