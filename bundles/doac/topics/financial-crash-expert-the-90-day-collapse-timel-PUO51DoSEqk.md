# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett talks with "Professor Steve" — the Australian-born economist Steve Keen, a specialist in the history of economic thought, financial instability, and the dynamics of money who is known for predicting the 2008 crash — about the war on Iran that he says the United States and Israel have started and are losing.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk] The guest's central claim is that the conflict is not primarily an oil-price story: Iran's blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, a 21 km choke point, threatens 20-30% of the world's fertilizer, roughly 30% of its helium (essential for semiconductors), a quarter of its liquefied natural gas, and much of its oil — and losing the fertilizer alone could trigger the first genuinely global famine.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]

Keen frames Trump's conduct as a "pump and dump" scheme — moving oil markets with announcements to enrich himself and his circle — and attributes the war to Israel's decades-long ambition to destroy Iran combined with a president he describes in terms of narcissistic personality disorder.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk] He walks through five scenarios for how the war could end, from Iran being destroyed with nuclear weapons (which he puts under 10% probability but calls civilization-ending) to his preferred and, in his view, most likely outcome: Iran disabling Israel's nuclear arsenal, taking the "Samson doctrine" off the table.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]

The conversation then pivots to economics: Keen argues the AI boom is a classic Schumpeterian boom-bust cycle heading for a severe contraction within roughly 24 months, that AI and robotics could eliminate up to half of working-class jobs and make universal basic income a necessity, that Bitcoin will ultimately go to zero on energy grounds, and that Western capitalism's pure-competition model needs rebalancing toward cooperation and long-term planning.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]

# Key points

- Keen argues the Strait of Hormuz is the war's real stake: a 21 km choke point through which oil, 20-30% of world fertilizer, sulfuric acid, and helium must pass, and which Iran now controls, deciding which countries' ships pass "depending on your country's attitude towards our country."[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- He claims losing 20% of world fertilizer means losing roughly 20% of world food — a global famine "we've never had before" — noting that without the Haber-Bosch process the planet could support only one to two billion people, and predicting India runs out of fertilizer within 2-3 months.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- About 30% of the world's helium comes from a gas field spanning Saudi Arabia and Iran; because helium cannot be stockpiled and is irreplaceable in chipmaking, the episode cites helium expert Phil Kornbluth forecasting a 2-3 month production shutdown, with South Korea — which gets 65% of its helium from Qatar and makes two-thirds of the world's memory chips — launching an emergency investigation.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- Keen presents a chart showing annual changes in gross world product and gross energy consumption moving in near lockstep, arguing a 5-10% fall in energy from the conflict means a comparable fall in global output; Australia, he notes, holds only about 30 days of oil supply.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- He accuses Trump of running a "pump and dump" on oil markets — making announcements that move prices and tipping off friends — and describes the US president as "a mafia don" whose sole nuclear launch authority (illustrated by an Annie Jacobsen clip) makes the situation uniquely dangerous.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- Iran's preparedness, in his account, includes dividing its military into 31 autonomous provincial commands to survive decapitation attacks, and hiding missile facilities hundreds of meters underground — making a country more than half the size of Western Europe with 90 million people effectively unconquerable, and any US ground invasion (which he rates at over 50% likely) "a suicide mission."[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- His five end-scenarios: Iran destroyed (requiring nuclear weapons, under 10% probability, "we're all gone"); Iran destroys Gulf power infrastructure, rendering Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Dubai uninhabitable (highly likely — one Saudi strike already removed two of 14 LNG units, a five-year rebuild); Israel invokes the "Samson doctrine" if facing existential defeat; Iran disables Israel's nukes (his hoped-for and most probable outcome); or Iran develops nuclear weapons of its own.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- He cites Dubai losing roughly $1 million per minute — about $1.4 billion a day — from unplanned airport shutdowns after Iranian drone incursions, arguing Iran is deliberately using economic pressure on Gulf states to force Trump's hand.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- On the AI economy, Keen invokes Schumpeter's boom-bust pattern (railways as precedent): big tech is on track to spend about $720 billion on AI infrastructure in 2026 against roughly a 5:1 spend-to-revenue ratio, AI-startup failure rates have hit 90%, and both he and Bartlett expect a severe contraction within about 24 months.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- He argues AI and robotics could wipe out up to 50% of working-class jobs — citing an Anthropic report showing a 13% decline in entry-level hiring — making universal basic income "a necessity," and frames the long-run choice as a "Star Trek future" of abundance versus a "Hunger Games" future where a robot-owning elite oppresses everyone else.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- Keen predicts Bitcoin goes to zero because its proof-of-work energy demands will be unaffordable once biosphere constraints force cuts to energy consumption — cryptocurrencies and international travel being, in his view, the easiest things to cut.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
- His prescriptions: individuals should pursue self-sufficiency (home solar, growing food where possible); societies should "stop electing fools," rebalance competition with cooperation (citing Cadbury and Mondragon as socialist-cooperative enterprises and China's hybrid system as directionally better), and respect planetary physical limits to avoid a "Seneca cliff" collapse.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]

# Takeaways

Keen's overarching argument is that modern civilization is far more fragile than its citizens or economists appreciate: a single regional war can sever the fertilizer, helium, and energy flows the entire production system quietly depends on, and mainstream economics — by ignoring money, energy, and physical resources — has left both leaders and the public blind to that fragility.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk] Whether through the Iran conflict, the AI bubble, or biosphere overshoot, he contends the system is heading for correction, and that resilience — personal self-sufficiency and a cooperative, long-term economic philosophy — matters more than individual financial maneuvering, which he says cannot save anyone inside an unsustainable system.[^financial-crash-expert-the-90-day-collapse-timel-PUO51DoSEqk]
