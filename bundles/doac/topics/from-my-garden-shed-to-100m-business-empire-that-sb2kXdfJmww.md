# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews George Heaton, who founded the luxury streetwear brand Represent with his older brother Michael in 2012 while still at college in Bolton, working out of their father's garden shed.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] Heaton describes a childhood shaped by a stoic, unemotional father who sold mini buses and drove a Range Rover, a mother who told him nightly that he had to "carry" the family, and a tailor grandmother whose fussiness about fit and fabric composition he credits for the brand's obsession with quality.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] He says he rejected the graphic-design career path — his teacher quoted an average salary of around £30k against £9k-a-year tuition — because he wanted the life his father had, and set out to build "the British version" of American graphic T-shirt brands like Obey and Diamond Supply.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]

The through-line of the conversation is a plateau and a reinvention. Heaton says the business climbed to roughly £6-7m and then sat flat for years while he and a team made up of school friends assumed that was the ceiling; at the same time a European company that owned the "Represent" trademark for clothing sent a letter demanding everything they had, a threat that hung over the business from 2018 until a multi-million-pound settlement in March 2020.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] He describes that stretch as rock bottom — an unprofitable year, a break-up, a body and self-image he disliked — and says he responded by drawing a picture of the person he wanted to become, adopting Andy Frisella's 75 Hard challenge, and reading self-improvement books nightly.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]

The business reinvention ran in parallel: stripping back wholesale to focus on direct-to-consumer weekly drops, moving production to Portugal, wiping social media and relaunching, and eventually hiring an experienced CEO from Puma.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] Heaton's central claim is the one he states outright — you do not need to be good at business to own a business — and his stated aim now is for Represent to be a lifestyle rather than a clothing label, with the clothes as a byproduct.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]

# Key points

- Heaton gives the early revenue curve as roughly £10,000 in year one, about £50k in year two, and around half a million in year three, when they incorporated and hired an accountant who told them to continue only if they had £100k in the bank — they did.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- He says he took no salary for seven or eight years, and that the business then sat flat at about £6-7m through roughly 2015-2017 because, in his words, they had limited beliefs and did not know what they did not know.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- On the "shedding phase" Bartlett describes, Heaton recalls a Facebook message when his Audi R8 broke down at 19 or 20 telling him the car "your dad bought you" had packed up — he says the mockery made him want to exceed his father's success a hundredfold.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- His hiring advice is "hire fast, fire faster", and he says his biggest early mistake was hiring only friends and only people who looked like they belonged in streetwear, when far better operators existed outside that mould.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- He argues small companies should pay for exceptional senior people now rather than wait: bringing in a CEO who had run a $500m business showed him geographies, product lifetimes and category expansion that took Represent from about $20m toward $50m — knowledge he says he could never have found inside his own peer group.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- The turnaround package was operational: killing wholesale for DTC weekly drops, chief product officer James moving production from the UK and Italy to Portugal (over Heaton's initial objections, transforming margin), Michael's new graphics, and a full website and social reset — sales went from 10-15 a day to 300 T-shirts in a minute, then 600, then 900.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- Heaton says the trademark letter was the worst day in the business: a European holder of the Section 25 clothing trademark demanded more money than the company had, only he, Michael and James knew, and the three-month gaps between the other side's lawyers' replies created what he calls a dark cloud that affected every decision, down to buying a sandwich.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- He reframes that threat as fuel — he began running in 2020 telling himself that stopping mid-15K meant "him coming for us" — and now says the fight catapulted the business by pushing it beyond T-shirts into a lifestyle brand.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- On personal change, he says people dismiss transformation as genetics or steroids (as he once did when overweight), and that the real starting point is incremental: 10,000 steps or a 20-minute workout, sustained long enough to see the small gains that create obsession.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- He rejects work-life balance outright, arguing that building something lasting "is going to take everything" and citing Kobe Bryant shooting free throws at 3am; he says he is willing to sacrifice everything, including for now having a family, and calls the word "toxic" applied to hard work meaningless.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- He is candid about the cost: a decade with essentially no life outside Represent, Sundays spent "pretending to work" because everyone else was with family, romantic relationships he avoided entirely until recently, and guilt-driven anxiety after spending four hours on a date.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- On defensibility he argues competitors can copy a logo or a design but not the brand's essence — the Owners Club community, run clubs and lifestyle — and says Represent is not yet at mainstream-dilution risk (a Burberry-style life cycle he places around $250-300m revenue), with only about 20% of revenue in the US.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- Heaton names discipline over motivation as the operating principle — motivation lasts minutes, discipline is a written instruction manual of principles he still gets as daily phone reminders — and says the brand's DNA must stay consistent through fashion cycles rather than chase trends.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
- Plans he outlines for the year include launching womenswear, opening the brand's first three physical stores as community spaces, scaling the 247 fitness line with possible gyms later, and building Cadence, a high-sodium ready-to-drink electrolyte can.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]

# Takeaways

Heaton's argument is that founder self-awareness, not business skill, is what unlocks scale: he says his single best decision was hiring a CEO and getting out of the way, because the operational side drained him within 15 minutes of a board meeting while he could design for 12 hours straight.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] He presents no exit strategy — he says he does not want one, that the mission has no finish line, and that if his brother Michael ever left he is not sure he would continue at all.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww] His advice to aspiring founders is to expect roughly ten years, to research openly available material rather than buy courses, and to accept that the same people rarely take a company from £50m to £250m to a billion.[^from-my-garden-shed-to-100m-business-empire-that-sb2kXdfJmww]
