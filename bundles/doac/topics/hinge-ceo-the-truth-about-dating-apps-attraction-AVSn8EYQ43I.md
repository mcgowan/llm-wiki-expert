# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews Justin McLeod, founder and CEO of Hinge, who says the app now sets up a date roughly every two seconds and has produced millions of relationships and marriages.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] McLeod traces the company back to his own life rather than to a market analysis: an only child of an entrepreneur father, good at maths and coding, who by the end of his freshman year had not gone to bed sober since term began, spent a summer in rehab, and got sober the day he graduated because, he says, "the steering wheel of my life was broken".[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] Sober and socially awkward at Harvard Business School, he built a Facebook app so students could list crushes after the school scrapped its "last chance dance" — and says the idea of connecting friends of friends then "infected" him in a way his other business-plan ideas never did.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]

McLeod frames Hinge's history as a sequence of near-death reboots. The original Facebook app, Secret Agent Cupid, was overcomplicated and running out of money; he threw it away, rebuilt for mobile, and spent his last $25,000 on a Washington DC launch party where the app had still not cleared App Store review the night before — it was approved the next morning, 2,500 people came, and the app made more matches in a night than in its entire history.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] A second reboot followed a 2015 Vanity Fair article, "the dawn of the dating apocalypse", which featured Hinge; McLeod says he told his head of marketing he wished he could start over, was asked what was stopping him, then let go of half the company and rebuilt around long-term relationships.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]

The personal story that reshaped the product is his reunion with Kate, his college girlfriend. Prompted by a journalist, Deborah, who told him he could still "do something dramatic", he flew to Zurich a month before Kate's wedding; they talked for seven or eight hours, she called off the wedding, and moved into his 300-square-foot West Village apartment.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] McLeod says the honeymoon lasted about three months, and that had they merely been dating he would have run — realising how many people get "skated past" is what convinced him a relationship app must slow people down and force vulnerability.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]

# Key points

- McLeod says early investors and classmates uniformly rejected the idea: the business plan competition did not place, a class paper was called a horrible idea, and VCs told him match.com owned a saturated market — he countered that a market nobody he knew used could not be saturated, and that the real problem was stigma.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- He argues the 2013 launch party worked because the "cool" DC social scene downloaded the app in front of each other, jump-starting past the stigma that online dating was for "lonely weirdos"; 400 people logged in unprompted the next day, then the effect snowballed city by city.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- Tinder's near-simultaneous launch helped rather than hurt, he says, by creating the category — Hinge's friends-of-friends model read as higher intent, and he went from begging for money to turning away investors in a nearly $20m round.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- The 2016 reboot deliberately traded engagement metrics for a single North Star — are we getting people out on good dates — and user numbers fell from roughly 400,000-500,000 to 100,000-150,000; McLeod says he took 50 or 60 VC meetings without a single yes before Match Group invested with about a week or two of cash left, later acquiring the company in 2019.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- Hinge Labs, which McLeod says is unusual among dating apps, runs research studies comparing successful and unsuccessful daters so the company can coach users rather than only adjust product design.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- Asked how to be the world's worst dating app user, he lists filtered photos in sunglasses, group shots with lots of friends, one-word prompt answers, liking everyone or no one, and waiting passively for likes to arrive.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- His case for vulnerability is practical: honesty gets you to an accurate mutual assessment faster and filters out people attracted to a veneer, and imperfections give others "hooks to grab onto" because people connect over cracks rather than over polish.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- On serial daters he says he was one — a string of two- to six-week relationships — and blames an over-narrow mental model (over 6ft, a particular job) that turns dating into a search for reasons to say no; his advice is to widen the aperture and see things through.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- Bartlett cites a 2021 Hinge study finding the top 1% of men receive more than 16% of all likes versus just over 11% for the top 1% of women, and a 2022 Bumble study showing men send about 13 messages a day to women's roughly three; McLeod says an internal effort called "flatten the power curve" aims to level up struggling daters and cap over-liking.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- He says the reboot cut the effort to get a date from about a thousand swipes to roughly 50 likes, and that the next leap is AI moving Hinge toward a matchmaker model — learning who you are, setting up dates directly, and following up afterwards, rather than making users write personal advertisements.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- The tagline "designed to be deleted" came from a branding exercise where McLeod refused to name a gimmick; he argues the business model works because word of mouth from good dates is the cheapest growth, and single people will always exist.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- Hinge's stated mission is to foster intimate connection to create a less lonely world; McLeod contrasts this with social networks that drifted into social media because brands, influencers and outrage hold attention better than friends do, and Bartlett cites 52% of Americans reporting loneliness and 57% eating meals alone.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- The culture principles from Hinge's book "How We Do Things" — designed to be deleted, radical trust, love the leap, guided by principles, people with heart — were reverse-engineered from post-mortems of what went wrong; McLeod says radical trust no longer fits a 300-person company because pushing decisions all the way down created silos and micro-optimisation, so he now pulls coordination back in.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
- On hiring, Hinge profiled who succeeded and who did not, built a "culture interview" everyone still takes, and screens for three core values — authenticity, courage and empathy — which McLeod credits for extraordinarily low voluntary attrition.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]

# Takeaways

McLeod's argument is that a dating app optimised for engagement and one optimised for outcomes look completely different, and that choosing outcomes meant accepting a shrinking user base, a near-empty bank account and years of investor rejection before word of mouth compounded.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] For individual daters, his prescription is the same principle applied personally: be honest and specific about who you are, be thoughtful with likes so the algorithm can learn your taste, and widen the narrow checklist that makes you reject people you have not yet had a chance to know.[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I] Asked what he would tell his ten-year-old self, he says he would want to convey intrinsic worth — then adds that not having it is what produced both Hinge and his marriage, so the advice becomes "buckle up".[^hinge-ceo-the-truth-about-dating-apps-attraction-AVSn8EYQ43I]
