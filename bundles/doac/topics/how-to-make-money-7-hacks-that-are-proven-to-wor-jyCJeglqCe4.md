# Overview

This episode is not a single interview but a New Year compilation: Steven Bartlett explains that 53% of his audience plan to make a money-related resolution, so the show pulled the most replayed and most shared money moments from its back catalogue into one sequence covering saving, spending, investing, tax, crypto, buying a house and money mindset.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] The through-line the guests keep returning to is stated early by Alex Hormozi: "the most expensive thing that all of us are paying for is the information that we don't know" — that the games of leverage, deal structuring and tax are played openly by the wealthy and are simply invisible to everyone else.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]

The first movement is mechanical and deliberately unglamorous. Ramit Sethi argues that ordinary people never get the investing conversation at all — Bartlett notes that among his own team of roughly 30 people, nobody had had it — and that the fix is one target-date fund, an automatic monthly transfer of 5-10% of take-home pay, and then leaving it alone.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] Morgan Housel extends the same logic into a theory of compounding in which endurance, not skill, is the variable to maximise.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] The second movement is about escaping the earner's trap: Hormozi and Codie Sanchez on stacking leverage and structuring deals for upside, Scott Galloway on why the tax code rewards owners over even very high earners, and Jaspreet Singh on the mindset and spending discipline that has to come before any of it works.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]

Two segments sit slightly apart from the rest. A crypto and blockchain segment makes the case for tokens as the first globally homogeneous, fractional asset that anyone can buy without gatekeepers.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] And Housel takes a deliberately contrarian line on property, arguing that buying a house is a lifestyle decision that has been badly miscast as an investment.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]

# Key points

- Sethi's simplest advice, the one he says he gives his own family, is a single target-date fund named for your retirement year (a Vanguard, Fidelity or Schwab 2065 fund, for example), which is automatically diversified and rebalances more conservatively as you age; he tells people to use a low-cost brokerage, avoid apps that gamify trading, and check the balance only every three to six months, on desktop, changing nothing.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Sethi's account-structure fix for people who raid their investments: pay flows into a checking account, then automatically out to savings sub-accounts for specific goals, to the investment account, and to guilt-free spending, with the credit card paid in full monthly — a setup that takes a couple of weeks to build and then never needs thinking about again; he says of anyone claiming they cannot afford to invest, "show me where you're spending your money, I guarantee you I can find 5%."[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- The compounding demonstration run live on air: about a hundred years of US stock market data give roughly 10-11% nominal returns, or 7-8% after inflation; using 7% and a money-chimp style calculator, $5,000 invested at 16 plus $5,000 a year becomes about $133,537 by age 30, $336,000 by 40, $736,000 by 50, and — averaging $30,000 a year across 49 years to age 65 — around $12.3 million (or $17.4 million at 8%, a number Sethi warns against counting on).[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Hormozi's leverage ladder, which he credits to Naval Ravikant's four types of leverage, is his stated blueprint for wealth: labour (working for someone, then yourself, then having others work for you), media (build once, license infinitely), capital (returns without sacrificing time), and technology (code written once and used by millions) — his own income moved from four to five to six figures a month as he climbed it, and the aim is to stack as many types, to as great a degree, as possible.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Hormozi's whiteboard exercise for finding "your unfair bet": list your skills on one side and money on the other, then match the skills to the sector with the biggest problem and highest-value solution, checking average revenue and profit margin online — he contrasts a biotech company at 50-80% margins and hundreds of millions in revenue with Walmart's roughly 6% margins or a trade services business, and tells the story of the father, the son and the old car to make the point that value depends on who you take it to.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- The specialisation example worked through live: a generic entry-level copywriter at Bartlett's social media company might earn about £25,000, while a medical writer — a skill he argues could be learned in a month by someone who can already write — commanded $150,000 or more and still could not be hired; Hormozi adds deal structure as a third lever, suggesting a writer take $10,000 of a $35,000 salary plus 10% of revenue driven above the existing baseline.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Bartlett's own illustration of deal structure: he took equity rather than salary in a psychedelics biotech, receiving roughly 400,000 shares, and when the company IPO'd on the NASDAQ at $3.2 billion his equity was worth close to $10 million for about six months of part-time work — his conclusion being that any young person with social media skills who knew how to structure a deal could have asked for stock instead of a fee.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Sanchez argues deal-making is "the most valuable skill set in the world" and that it is no longer gatekept — she contrasts her own early private equity career, when she was not allowed in the rooms where terms were set, with today's freely available information, and points listeners to her book *Main Street Millionaire* and Contrarian Thinking's courses and newsletters.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Galloway's tax argument is that the code has grown from 400 pages to 4,000 and the extra 3,600 pages exist "to turn rich people into super rich people": the wealthiest 25 Americans pay roughly 6-8% in tax via buy-borrow-against-it-and-die, state arbitrage (he is scathing about Jeff Bezos's move to Florida), and provisions like Section 1202 qualified small business stock, under which his first $10 million from selling his analytics company L2 and the first $25 million from a bankruptcy turnaround came out tax-free.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Galloway's resulting rule is "don't be a super earner, be a super owner": a lawyer and chiropractor couple earning $1.6 million in a blue-state city can pay 45-52%, while the same money made by buying and selling assets is taxed far less; he says he consults his tax adviser before setting up a company or taking a large payment, calls tax avoidance an obligation pursued legally, and simultaneously says he would vote for an alternative minimum tax and higher corporate rates because this is "how we screw over the little guy."[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Housel says his entire net worth is cash, a house, index funds and some Markel shares held as a board member, in one bank account and one brokerage account; he does not claim nobody can beat the market, but says the variable he maximises is endurance — "if I can just earn average returns for an above average period of time" it puts you in the top 5% of investors — and jokes that successful investing is when you have forgotten the password to your account.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Housel's evidence for endurance: 99% of Warren Buffett's net worth was accumulated after his 60th birthday (roughly $3 billion at 60 versus over $100 billion later), so the story is 80 years of continuous compounding rather than stock-picking genius; and Ronald James Read, the janitor who died in 2014 aged 92 leaving over $8 million, simply bought stocks with small savings and left them alone for 70 years — his parents, dollar-cost averaging into index funds for 40 years without ever selling, would rank in the top 3% of professional investors.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- On property, Housel says if you are buying a house because you think it is a good financial investment, "run for your life": Robert Shiller's Nobel-recognised work shows US home prices flat in inflation-adjusted terms over 150 years, and only the last 20-30 years inflated expectations; he owns a home for family stability and says he would not care if he lost money on it, adding that the old assumption that rentals are poor housing no longer holds in most big cities.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- The blockchain segment frames the technology as being about truth rather than money — a public "database in the sky" verified by everyone, removing the need to trust a bank or government — and argues its investment case rests on incentives: miners and verifiers are rewarded for maintaining a scarce asset (Bitcoin capped at 21 million), and unlike AI, which retail investors cannot access without accreditation, tokens are fractional, globally homogeneous and identical in Nigeria, Brazil, London or India; self-custody via a hardware wallet and a seed phrase is presented as money no government can freeze and no border can stop, contrasted with Jewish families smuggling gold and diamonds out of Nazi Germany.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Singh's diagnosis for anyone living paycheck to paycheck is that they are the ideal customer for the whole system — banks sell them payday loans and credit cards, corporations sell them status goods, and the government taxes employees most heavily — so the first move is to stop making everyone else rich; he defines a "financial danger zone" as having under $2,000 saved plus credit card debt, and prescribes cutting restaurants, holidays and even Netflix, not to save $15 a month but to reclaim the two-plus hours a day the average American spends watching television for learning and earning.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
- Singh and Bartlett both frame the cost as seasons rather than permanent deprivation — "you have to pick your hard, either life's going to be hard now or it's going to be hard for the rest of your life" — with Bartlett describing his own £1,150-a-month room in Moss Side, the difficulty of dating while broke, and reckless spending as a dopamine substitute for a miserable life; Singh notes he drove a $500 car without a bumper the first year he made a million dollars, and warns that the desire to look rich is exactly what makes people prey for get-rich-quick schemes.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]

# Takeaways

Taken together, the guests describe two different games that have to be played in sequence. The first is defensive and dull: stop the leaks, automate a fixed percentage into a diversified fund, and then let time rather than cleverness do the work — Sethi's turkey that must not be fiddled with, Housel's forgotten password, Ronald Read's 70 years of patience.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] The second is offensive and informational: Hormozi's leverage stack, Sanchez's deal structuring and Galloway's insistence that the tax code rewards owners rather than earners all rest on the same claim — that the returns come from knowing which room you are in, and that the ignorance is now self-inflicted because nobody is gatekeeping the information any more.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4] Singh supplies the precondition the others assume: none of the mechanics matter until the emotional spending that keeps people broke has been confronted, and the guests are consistent that this means a bounded season of sacrifice rather than a life of it.[^how-to-make-money-7-hacks-that-are-proven-to-wor-jyCJeglqCe4]
