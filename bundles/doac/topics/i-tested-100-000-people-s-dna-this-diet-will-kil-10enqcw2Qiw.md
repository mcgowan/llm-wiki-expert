# Overview

In this return appearance on The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews Gary Brecka, a human biologist — he is explicit that he is not a physician and not licensed to practise medicine — who spent roughly 20 years on a mortality team predicting life expectancy for life insurance and annuity companies before founding the wellness firm Streamline.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw] His organising analogy is botanical: a real arborist confronted with a rotting palm leaf does not touch the leaf, he core-tests the soil and adds the missing nitrogen — and Brecka argues medicine has stopped thinking about people that way, treating disease as something that happens to us rather than within us.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]

The central mechanism he describes is methylation, the chain of conversions by which, for example, folate from green leafy vegetables becomes methylfolate, which downregulates the inflammatory amino acid homocysteine, which becomes methionine, which he says helps quiet the mind.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw] Where a gene in that chain is broken, he argues, you cannot fix the gene but you can supplement for its function cheaply — and he says his company processes about 20,000 genetic methylation tests a month, often alongside roughly 74 blood biomarkers, giving him a data set he treats as his main evidential authority.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]

Brecka frames his career change as moral rather than commercial. He describes reading thousands of medical records in which people became real to him — a husband's death, then antidepressants appearing, then body mass changing — and being legally barred from contacting them, at one point threatened with prosecution for wanting to warn a patient about a contraindicated drug interaction he believed would cause a clot.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw] He says the job felt like "sitting behind a thick glass wall just watching blind people walk into traffic", and that he found his purpose in that pain.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]

# Key points

- Brecka says everyone should run three basic panels — a glycemic profile (glucose, HbA1c, insulin), a hormone panel including DHEA and SHBG, and basic nutrient markers (vitamin D3, magnesium, potassium, B12) — plus one lifetime genetic methylation test covering MTHFR, MTR, MTRR, AHCY and COMT.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- He calls MTHFR "the most common gene mutation in the world", says 44% of the population carries it, and describes it as an inability to convert folic acid and folate into usable methylfolate — arguing it is methylfolate rather than folic acid that prevents neural tube defects.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- His account of anxiety is biochemical: catecholamines (norepinephrine, epinephrine, ephedrone, dopamine) rising without a trigger because the person is deficient in methylated B vitamins, specifically methylcobalamin and methylfolate; he says the COMT gene splits people into fast and slow breakers-down, nicknamed "warrior" and "worrier".[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- He offers hallmarks distinguishing this from a true anxiety disorder: no identifiable trigger, presence on and off since childhood, an active mind at night in a tired body, defaulting to worst-case scenarios, and anti-anxiety medication that mostly produces a zombie feeling.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- He reframes ADD and ADHD as attention overload rather than attention deficit — an overactive mind that should be quieted rather than a nervous system to be stimulated with amphetamines to match its pace.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- From his testing data he estimates 50% of people are clinically deficient in vitamin D3 (higher with darker complexions), most are B12 deficient below 500, and 25-40% show hormone levels outside optimal range — usually, he argues, from nutrient deficiency rather than endocrine disease, correctable with DHEA and D3 rather than exogenous hormones.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- On blood pressure he notes 85% of hypertension diagnoses are idiopathic, points to Journal of Hypertension work linking urinary homocysteine to cardiovascular disease, and says thousands of times he has seen trimethylglycine lower homocysteine and normalise a patient's blood pressure at their next doctor's visit.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- The "diet that will kill you" is his case against refined carbohydrates: he argues pre-diabetes is a pandemic driven not only by obvious sugar but by white flour, rice, bread, pasta and potatoes overpowering the pancreas, and calls blood sugar "the root of all evil".[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- He is sceptical of statins, arguing studies treat LDL in a vacuum while lowering the body's capacity to make hormones and cell membranes, and says that among hundreds of death claims he processed he cannot recall a single centenarian who did not have clinically elevated LDL at death.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- He describes corticosteroids as anti-inflammatory at first but eating the joint "like a termite", to the point that his mortality models could schedule a joint replacement about six years out, artificially advance the person's age, and cascade in every disease that worsens with reduced mobility.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- His five daily commitments are mineralised water on waking (10oz with Baja Gold or Celtic sea salt, which he ranks above pink Himalayan and far above table salt), an omega-3 DHA/EPA supplement, then sunlight, grounding and breath work — he does three rounds of 30 Wim Hof-style breaths every morning and says he never misses.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- His stated reason for that consistency is psychological rather than biochemical: he argues breaking small private promises to yourself erodes self-confidence and the ability to trust yourself, and that the body craves the same-time-every-day signal.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- On grounding he claims bare skin on soil, grass or sand exchanges ions with the earth's low Gauss current, repolarising red blood cells so they repel rather than clump — a change he says is visible under a microscope within ten minutes and reproducible with a PEMF mat costing around $5,000.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- On red light (photobiomodulation) he says wavelengths around 600-1,000nm displace mitochondrial nitric oxide and force oxygen to dock in the Krebs cycle, shifting output from 2 to 36 units of ATP, and reports Joe Rogan no longer needing reading glasses after using a bed Brecka installed; he adds that first light — the first 45 minutes of day, with no UVA or UVB — is the free version.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- On Ozempic he says semaglutide and tirzepatide can be life-changing for type 2 diabetics and the morbidly obese but have become "drugs for vanity", citing gastric emptying and paralytic gut, a third to half of lost weight being lean mass, and facial fat loss — and insists anyone using them should be strength training, with his clinical team adding growth hormone peptides like sermorelin or ipamorelin.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
- From the mortality data he states as "a material fact" that isolating a human being at any age halves their life expectancy, describes the well-documented "broken heart syndrome" in bereaved elderly couples, and notes that across blue zones the diets differed wildly (Singapore high meat, Sardinia high bread and pasta) while mobility into old age and a sense of purpose were the constants.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]

# Takeaways

Brecka's core claim is that people have far more data on their businesses than on their bodies, and that supplementing without testing is "paralysis of analysis" — the point of the methylation test and the three blood panels is to supplement for a known deficiency rather than for its own sake.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw] He is open about the criticism he attracts, acknowledging he has made mistakes such as quoting articles rather than research, saying he has become more guarded and precise, and repeating that he has never claimed to be a doctor.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw] His stated ambition is that the mortality databases used by insurers — which he says know the day, date, time, location and cause of death for millions — would see daylight, because in his view they would upend modern medicine.[^i-tested-100-000-people-s-dna-this-diet-will-kil-10enqcw2Qiw]
