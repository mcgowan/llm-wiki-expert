# Overview

In this Diary Of A CEO episode, metabolic and fat-cell scientist Dr. Benjamin Bikman returns to argue that the decades-old "eat less, exercise more" calorie-centric model of weight loss is the wrong first step: calories are "relevant, but not the most relevant," and the variable that actually governs fat storage is the hormone insulin, which he calls "the one metabolic hormone to rule them all" because it tells every cell in the body what to do with energy.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc] The guest contends that calorie-restriction diets fail because insulin-spiking meals lower the brain's two fuels (glucose and ketones), driving hunger even when fat cells are full — and that lowering insulin by controlling carbohydrates lets people eat protein and fat freely without the "gnawing hunger" that dooms most diets.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]

The episode's title claim rests on the guest's account of the liver: in a low-insulin state (fasting or low-carb), fat cells release fatty acids to the liver, which "cannot store fat" and must burn it, producing ketones — he cites a study in which a week of ketogenic eating reduced liver fat by roughly 60% in people with fatty liver disease, on the same calories as a high-carb comparison diet.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc] Much of the conversation is a tour of ketone science — exogenous ketone drinks (host Steven Bartlett, a Ketone IQ investor, takes a shot on air and tracks his blood ketones rising from 0.3 to 0.9 mmol), ketones' effects on the brain, heart failure, blood pressure, and anxiety — plus the guest's prescribed "perfect 2026 plan": no carbs before dinner, a big protein-and-fat lunch, fasted resistance training, no evening snacking, and family dinner eaten flexibly.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]

The pair also address counterarguments: a listener's account of her husband whose undiagnosed insulinoma made a keto attempt nearly fatal (the guest calls insulinoma patients "the worst person" for low-carb), the debate over keto for women, GLP-1 drugs' two-year fade and lean-mass loss, and the BBC's criticism of the show's cancer-metabolism guest Thomas Seyfried.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]

# Key points

- The guest asserts that without insulin it is "completely impossible" to get fat, citing type 1 diabetes: untreated patients waste away regardless of intake, some abuse this by deliberately underdosing insulin to stay thin, and starting insulin therapy can add 10-15 pounds within days.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- He cites Harvard's Dr. David Ludwig's isocaloric-meal work: insulin-spiking meals reduce "total energy availability" in the blood, and high-carb breakfasts leave people hungrier sooner than calorie-identical low-carb ones — the mechanism, he argues, behind post-carb brain fog and cravings.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- Liposuction is his proof that fat mass alone is not the issue: removing 20 pounds of fat cells improves no health marker — "if they were diabetic, they're just as diabetic" — because metabolic health depends on shrinking fat cells hormonally, not removing them.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- His lab published findings that ketones triple the metabolic rate of fat tissue (shown in petri-dish cells, animal models, and human belly-fat biopsies), and in ketosis the body literally wastes calories by breathing and urinating out ketones — a "metabolic advantage" over isocaloric high-carb eating.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- Ketones are presented as the brain's preferred fuel, with claimed evidence of benefit across epilepsy, migraines, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, depression, dementia, and multiple sclerosis; BHB also acts as a signaling molecule and anxiolytic, and case studies suggest ketogenic diets can help eating disorders.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- On the heart, he describes a 2025 pig-heart study in which infused L-BHB dilated the aorta and great vessels so the heart ejected 40% more blood per beat, and shares an n=1 anecdote of drinking a few grams of L-BHB and seeing his own blood pressure drop from about 139/90 to 110/70 within an hour.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- On sex differences: women in the follicular phase are "fat burning machines" with free fatty acids 40-50% higher than men and enter ketosis faster, while the high-progesterone luteal phase raises hunger; he cites Dr. Isabella Cooper's crossover study showing no significant cortisol change when keto-adapted women switched to a 55%-carb diet and back, countering the claim that keto uniquely stresses women.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- His prescribed daily protocol: 5 a.m. weighted ruck, ice bath, yerba mate instead of breakfast, late-morning fasted resistance training plus sauna, a big protein-and-fat lunch, whatever the family eats at a 5-6 p.m. dinner, and — his single most important rule — no evening snacking, since going to bed after a glucose spike activates the sympathetic nervous system and wrecks sleep.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- On GLP-1 drugs (an Ozempic pen is produced on air): he says sweet-craving suppression fades by two years, about 70% of users quit by then, and roughly 40% of weight lost is lean mass (muscle and bone) that may never return in older users — so he advocates low-dose, 90-day cycles paired with low-carb coaching, citing an unpublished collaboration with an Idaho clinic that he says outperforms the drug alone; allulose and collagen peptides are offered as natural GLP-1 boosters.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- He endorses Thomas Seyfried's metabolic theory of cancer — nucleus-transfer experiments suggesting cancer follows the mitochondria, not the nucleus — and says his own ketogenic adherence is partly motivated by his mother's death from cancer; he and the host criticize the BBC's "misinformation" coverage of the show, with Bartlett claiming the flagged content was 0.001% of transcribed hours.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- Supplement views: creatine at 10-15 g/day for brain benefits (versus 5 g for muscle), especially for women given their higher Alzheimer's risk, with elevated creatinine on blood tests dismissed as an artifact rather than kidney damage; omega-3 for muscle building; ashwagandha for sleep and cortisol; and a speculative caution that over-consumed fortified B vitamins may make metabolism too "efficient" at storing fat, drawing on livestock-feed data.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
- Testing advice: he would add fasting insulin (target ~7 microunits/mL or less, enabling a HOMA score) to standard blood panels worldwide, recommends continuous glucose monitors as powerful behavior-change tools, and notes continuous ketone monitors are already available in Europe.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]

# Takeaways

The guest's closing advice to a struggling would-be dieter is behavioral as much as metabolic: find a reason beyond yourself (such as your children), attack the one habit you know is hurting you (evening snacking, alcohol), recruit outside help, and control cravings by keeping insulin low rather than fighting hunger head-on — "every diet works until you stop doing it."[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc] Both host and guest carry disclosed commercial interests (Bartlett is an investor in Ketone IQ, and the guest promotes his own site and collaborations), the guest repeatedly stresses he is not giving medical advice and that keto is not the only way to lose weight, and the insulinoma story stands as the episode's own warning that the approach can be dangerous for people with certain conditions.[^insulin-expert-how-to-drain-your-liver-of-fat-do-C7LL7VwP8Nc]
