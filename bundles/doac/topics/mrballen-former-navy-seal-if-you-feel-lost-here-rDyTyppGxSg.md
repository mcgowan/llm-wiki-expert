# Overview

John Allen — the former Navy SEAL who became the storyteller MrBallen — tells Steven Bartlett that every turn in his life traces back to a single moment of self-awareness in his mother's basement at 18, when he stopped blaming other people and accepted that his collapse was his own doing.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg] He describes growing up as the self-styled black sheep in an unusually accomplished family — a father with a Pulitzer Prize, a sister with two, another sister with a PhD who worked out of a Harvard lab, and a professional writer for a mother — and rebelling by being a deliberately bad student in working-class Quincy, Massachusetts.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]

From that basement, Allen says, the method was always the same: set one concrete goal, organize your whole life around it, and own everything that happens along the way. Graduating college became the first goal, becoming a Navy SEAL the second, and he argues that the specific goal matters far less than having one — invoking the military maxim that an 80% solution now beats a 100% solution tomorrow, and Jocko Willink's advice that a lost person simply needs to start moving.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg] His most unorthodox claim is that people should be honest in "shower thoughts" about their real motivation — fame, money, proving a parent wrong — because unexamined desires become "unchecked boxes" that produce deathbed regret whether or not society approves of them.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]

The conversation also covers the costs: a grenade wound in Afghanistan in 2014, a near-breakdown on a later deployment to Peru, a medical retirement he did not plan for, and the SEAL community's public rejection of him when he began posting SEAL content online.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg] Allen frames therapy, and the willingness to say deeply embarrassing things out loud, as the only durable way he has found to handle what he calls demons — the intrusive thoughts you cannot argue yourself out of.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]

# Key points

- Allen recounts finishing his first semester at the University of Massachusetts with a 1.016 GPA, taking part in a 2006 campus riot after a football loss, being identified from police photos in the subsequent hunt, and withdrawing at 18 rather than being expelled — after his mother, a professional writer, had written the college essay that got him admitted despite poor grades.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- He says his mother refused to cushion the landing, charging him rent and telling him to get a job or move out, and credits that refusal with letting him reach the realisation that the situation was his own fault.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- Allen describes SEAL training as roughly a two-year pipeline — two months of boot camp in Chicago, a two-month prep school, six months of BUD/S (basic underwater demolition SEAL school), then about six months of advanced qualification training and a further school — with BUD/S, especially its first two months, the stage where most candidates fail.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- On who survives, he argues the decorated arrivals wash out fastest — he cites a former Arizona Diamondbacks player who quit quickly — because instructors deliberately target anyone with a resume, while the "ragtag" candidates with an internal chip on their shoulder have something to hold onto when the discomfort peaks.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- He tells the story of failing a CS gas confidence exercise on San Clemente Island in the final phase of training — he bolted on instinct and had to be tackled — was publicly called out in front of his class, and was made to wear a marked garment over his shorts for the rest of training; he says openly owning it, rather than making excuses, is what earned his teammates' trust.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- Allen recounts being caught in a 2014 grenade blast in an Afghan alleyway, too weak to pull the tourniquets off his kit, expecting either to bleed out or be shot; the thought that struck him was acute sadness that he had not yet started a family, and he and his wife began one as soon as he got home.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- He describes a later six-month deployment to Peru — training Peruvian forces, not combat — as the worst period of his life, with a recurring dream of being abducted outside a Russian building and what he calls a borderline mental collapse, which surfaced unresolved trauma from Afghanistan and led to his medical retirement at the end of 2017.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- Job-hunting on LinkedIn after retirement, he was one of the few veterans to reply to a cold message from former investment banker Jordan, and the two co-founded Elite Meet, a charity running networking events between transitioning veterans and private-sector hiring managers.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- When he began posting SEAL-branded content in 2018-2019, Allen says he violated the community's code against trading on the SEAL identity for personal gain and was effectively disowned — receiving two to three deeply personal messages a day from people he had served with, and being confronted in Virginia Beach grocery stores — so he deleted nearly all of it.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- The pivot came from a 60-second video about the Dyatlov Pass mystery, filmed alone in a Pennsylvania water-park hotel room; it drew over five million views on an account with no followers, against roughly 100,000 across everything he had posted before, and landed just as TikTok surged at the start of the pandemic.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- He retells the Dyatlov case in detail — nine expert Soviet mountaineers found dead in the Urals, tents cut open from the inside, footprints in bare feet, clothing exchanged between men and women, trace radiation, one caved-in chest with no laceration, an officer's report of lights in the sky, and a case closed as "unknown unnatural force" — noting the 2020 reinvestigation's ice-slab conclusion leaves too much unexplained, while calling himself a skeptic.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- Allen says the early grind of five 25-minute videos a week, each taking him about 26 hours to make alone, left him barely sleeping, heavily overweight and drinking heavily, and that he later traded output for family time; he now estimates 20-25 million subscribers across all platforms, with about 10 million on the main channel.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- Citing Will Smith's account of skydiving — that the best things in life are on the other side of fear — he says the anticipation is always worse than the act, and that a 15-show live tour finally checked his oldest unchecked box after he once froze on stage in San Francisco and walked off; having checked it, he says he now has no big goal left and is looking for one.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- On mental health, Allen argues demons are the intrusive thoughts you cannot talk yourself out of, and that the only relief he has found is hearing himself say them aloud to a neutral third party; he credits his UK-based therapist Vinny Shoreman, and says therapy recently helped him reconcile with his father after roughly 14 years of estrangement rooted in a misread lack of belief in him.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
- On craft, he says great storytelling is delivery rather than script — inhabit the story rather than recite it — and that the reveal must be withheld for a payoff at the end rather than stated up front the way a newspaper would.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]

# Takeaways

Allen's argument to anyone who feels lost is a sequence rather than a philosophy: accept that the situation is at least partly your fault, resist paralysis by analysis, and pick a goal that genuinely checks a box for you even if the motive is one you would be embarrassed to admit — then move, because failure is feedback and opportunities only open for people already in motion.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg] Bartlett's reading of the story is that what looks in hindsight like extraordinary luck with timing was really a repeated willingness to act and to embarrass himself, and Allen's own coda is that the things too personal to say out loud are precisely the ones that will follow you until you say them.[^mrballen-former-navy-seal-if-you-feel-lost-here-rDyTyppGxSg]
