# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews Dr Nathan Bryan, a biochemist with a PhD in molecular and cellular physiology who spent years as a professor of molecular medicine at the University of Texas Health Science Center in Houston and has published over a hundred peer-reviewed papers on nitric oxide. Bryan argues that nitric oxide — a signalling gas produced in the endothelium that dilates blood vessels and regulates blood flow, oxygen delivery, stem cell mobilization, and cellular energy production — is foundational to human health, and that the loss of nitric oxide production is the earliest event in the onset and progression of age-related chronic disease.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]

Bryan traces his interest to two formative experiences: a dinner conversation with Lou Ignarro, who shared the 1998 Nobel Prize for the discovery of nitric oxide and told him that restoring nitric oxide production would "change the landscape of medicine", and his paraplegic, diabetic father's non-healing wound, which Bryan says he healed within six months using a topical nitric oxide product after four years of failed standard care.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]

Much of the conversation focuses on what Bryan says destroys nitric oxide production — antiseptic mouthwash, fluoride toothpaste, antacids (proton pump inhibitors), sugar, mouth breathing, and sedentary living — and what supports it, including nasal breathing, exercise, sunlight, humming, tongue scraping, and dietary nitrate. He also delivers a broader critique of for-profit medicine, arguing that the pharmaceutical business model is built on keeping customers on drugs rather than curing disease. His most striking claim is that he is "absolutely convinced" nitric oxide therapy will eradicate and cure Alzheimer's if administered early enough.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]

# Key points

- Bryan states that nitric oxide production declines roughly 10-12% per decade of life: by age 40 people have lost about 50% of their capacity, and he cites a Japanese study finding a 75% reduction in people aged 70-80 compared with 20-year-olds.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- He calls erectile dysfunction the "canary in the coal mine" — the first symptom of nitric oxide deficiency in both men and women and an accelerated form of cardiovascular disease; he notes 50% of US men over 40 self-report ED and believes the true figure is higher.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- The guest claims two out of three Americans have unsafely elevated blood pressure and that 50% of patients on blood pressure medication don't improve because ACE inhibitors, ARBs, and calcium channel blockers are not targeted at restoring nitric oxide — what he calls "resistant hypertension".[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- Bryan argues Alzheimer's is a vascular disease ("diabetes type three") and that nitric oxide addresses every root cause — blood flow, glucose uptake, inflammation, oxidative stress, and immune dysfunction — making him convinced it can cure the disease if patients are treated before an irreversible point of no return.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- He links nitric oxide to all three of his "hallmarks of longevity": it mobilizes pluripotent stem cells, activates telomerase to prevent telomere shortening, and signals cells to make more and better mitochondria — no other single molecule, he says, does all three.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- Humans lack the enzyme to reduce dietary nitrate from green leafy vegetables, so oral bacteria are 100% responsible for that conversion; killing the oral microbiome with antiseptic mouthwash means "zero" nitric oxide benefit from diet and, per data he cites, the loss of the cardioprotective benefits of exercise.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- In a 2019 study Bryan describes, seven days of twice-daily mouthwash use raised blood pressure in young healthy subjects — one 21-year-old's pressure rose 26 mmHg, which Bryan equates to a 26% increase in cardiovascular disease risk.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- He urges eliminating fluoride, citing US National Toxicology Program reporting that fluoride has no benefit and lowers IQ in children by as much as seven points while damaging thyroid function; he notes fluoride remains in most toothpaste and in 72% of US municipal water.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- Bryan claims that among his cancer contacts with solid primary tumors, "100%" have dental infections, and he references an NYU analysis of saliva from over 160,000 participants linking more than a dozen oral bacteria species to up to 50% higher risk of head and neck cancers — while conceding cause and effect is not established.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- He attacks proton pump inhibitors (omeprazole, Nexium, Prilosec) for blocking the stomach acid needed for nutrient absorption, tying this to widespread deficiencies (75% of Americans deficient in magnesium, 95% in iodine), and says sugar "glues" itself to the nitric oxide-producing enzyme, which is why diabetics have roughly 10 times higher rates of heart attack and stroke.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- Practical boosters he recommends: nasal breathing (the enzyme in sinus epithelium produces nitric oxide, which mouth breathing bypasses), humming at resonant frequencies, tongue scraping (which his research associates with a more diverse oral microbiome), 20-30 minutes of daily sunlight and red/infrared light, non-fluoridated toothpaste, and hormone optimization, since testosterone and estrogen both stimulate nitric oxide production.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
- On the medical system, Bryan argues that "no one is interested in curing human disease" because medicine is a trillion-dollar business built on lifetime customer value and polypharmacy, and he claims former FDA officials routinely move to highly paid pharma board seats; he notes the US spends nearly 20% of GDP on healthcare yet ranks last among high-income countries on outcomes, with Americans living about 13 years with disease.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]

# Takeaways

Bryan's central message is that nitric oxide deficiency, not bad luck, underlies fatigue, hypertension, ED, diabetes, and dementia — and that the decline is preventable: he claims that at 51 he has the vascular age of a 36-year-old by following his own principles. His prescription is mostly subtractive — stop using antiseptic mouthwash, fluoride, antacids, and sugar — combined with nasal breathing, exercise, sunlight, and a healthy oral microbiome. He cautions that nitric oxide can be overdone (dangerous blood pressure drops, methemoglobinemia) and that it is "not a silver bullet" for bad habits. Listeners should note that many of the episode's strongest claims — curing Alzheimer's, the tooth-organ "meridian" model, the cancer-dental infection link, and criticisms of fluoride and biohackers — are the guest's contested views, and that Bryan sells nitric oxide products through his company, a commercial interest he discusses openly in the episode.[^no-1-nitric-oxide-expert-why-youre-always-tired-zECoaEZRRFU]
