# Overview

Steven Bartlett interviews Dr. Susan Bratton, a sex educator and author of more than 30 books and programs who describes her brand of teaching as "heart-connected, conscious, passionate lovemaking" rather than therapy — she is emphatic that she is not a therapist and deals in technique, anatomy and bedroom communication rather than clinical treatment.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] Her route into the field was personal: twelve years into her marriage to her husband Tim, with a successful Silicon Valley career and a young daughter, she had never had an orgasm from intercourse, was giving what she calls "mercy sex" a couple of times a month, and assumed something was wrong with her.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] Childhood sexual trauma had left her dissociating during intimacy — physically present but emotionally checked out as protection against being hurt again — and she credits a combination of talk therapy, somatic work and being fully witnessed by a therapist with moving her through it.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]

Bratton's central argument is that the problem is almost never a broken person but an absent education: she says most couples are "the blind leading the blind" because film and pornography model only male-focused, friction-driven sex, so people never learn what is possible.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] The anatomical claim she treats as the biggest lever is that a female body contains the same volume of erectile tissue as a penis, arranged around the vaginal canal, and that it takes roughly 20 to 30 minutes of slow pleasuring for that tissue to engorge — so women told they "can't orgasm from intercourse" have, in her framing, simply been rushed rather than warmed up.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]

The episode is structured around audience questions voiced by actors — a mother in her forties who no longer recognises her body, a young man who lasts two or three minutes, a man stuck in a rigid weekly routine — with Bratton prescribing communication frameworks, "erotic play dates," bodywork techniques and sex-toy demonstrations for each.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] She also speaks openly about her own non-monogamy, having been in an open relationship with Tim for over 20 of their 33 years together, while stressing she is not advocating it for others and that STI testing is a non-negotiable precondition.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]

# Key points

- Bratton claims there are more than 20 kinds of orgasm, which she sorts into three families: locations touched (clitoral, vaginal, anal, breast, nipple, throat), techniques used (female ejaculation, expanded orgasm), and tools or objects of desire — and she argues all bodies, male and female, can access the full range because "we have the same parts, they're just an innie and an outie."[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- Her orgasm hierarchy runs from the "one-and-done" Masters-and-Johnson-style climax through multiple orgasms, to extended orgasm (staying inside the sensation), to what she calls the expanded or "quantum" orgasm — the "queen of orgasms" — which she describes as riding waves of climax for 10, 20 or 30 minutes under the lightest touch, and which she says she learned from her mentor Dr. Patti Taylor.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- The single technique she says men most need is to slow down: because male erection takes a minute or two while female engorgement takes 20-30 minutes, she recommends yoni massage — massaging the whole vulva with oil before intercourse, sometimes on a separate day with no intercourse at all — to bring numb or painful tissue online as pleasurable.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- She reports that when women are touched on the breasts, nipples, labia or clitoris and asked what they feel, the answers cluster into four categories — pain, numbness, shame, or pleasure — and argues that repeated safe, loving touch is what converts the first three into the fourth by building neural pathways.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- On the listener who lasts two or three minutes, Bratton says one in four men of all ages experience premature ejaculation or performance anxiety, and teaches the "ME breath" from Jim Benson's Multiorgasmic Lover program — a combination of PC-muscle squeeze, a Taoist "cool draw" breath, and a pelvic rock — practised during masturbation to build what she calls "ejaculatory choice."[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- Her core relationship formula, credited to author Dossie Easton, is that safety plus novelty equals desire: a partnership that is only safe becomes boring, so she prescribes a "sex life bucket list" of 48 ideas that partners each grade A, B or C and then work through together, starting with the mutual A-list.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- Two communication tools she names from her book Sexual Soulmates: the "Sexual Soulmate Pact," an agreement that either partner can say anything and the other will be glad to hear it rather than treat it as criticism, and a decades-long game she plays with Tim called "three things I love about you" in which a compliment can never be repeated.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- She rejects the foreplay/sex distinction as a product of religious repression that treats intercourse as the only real sex, and pushes the "platinum rule" over the golden rule — treat a partner the way their body needs to be treated, not the way you would want to be treated.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- For disconnected couples she prescribes starting with holding rather than conversation, on the grounds that oxytocin from being held is the antidote to chronic cortisol, followed by a "menu of small offers" — an explicitly no-pressure, no-intercourse-required evening — because, she argues, stress blocks arousal and pressure produces stress.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- Bratton separates three terms she says people confuse: libido is a function of overall health, desire is how you feel about yourself, and arousal is the physiological blood-flow process — which is why she says body-image work matters as much as technique for women who feel undesirable.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- She offers a hormonal framing for the body-image gap, claiming estrogen makes women judgmental — including toward themselves — while testosterone gives men "rose-coloured lenses," so "he thinks he looks better than he does, and she thinks she looks worse than she does."[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- On pornography she is not prohibitionist but dismissive, citing a Time magazine report of a study sampling roughly 340 porn clips that found 96% degrading to women in some way; she says she has met many men addicted to porn and none of the women, and prefers fantasy, toys and app-connected devices to watching "someone else's agenda."[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- She describes reframing her husband's affair, disclosed during their sexless period, not as cheating but as a man trying to stay in the marriage without being miserable — and credits the couple's recovery to therapy, sex workshops, and adopting Brad Blanton's radical-honesty practice of telling each other the full truth.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
- As chief advocacy officer for an at-home STI testing product, she states the caveat attached to all her encouragement of sexual adventure: test before going any further than kissing, because some infections carry permanent downstream consequences rather than an antibiotic and done.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]

# Takeaways

Bratton's through-line is that sexuality is a learnable skill set on the same footing as a career or a fitness practice, and that the "little hinge that swings a big door" is usually anatomical knowledge plus permission to talk honestly.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] Her practical prescriptions are consistent across every listener question in the episode: slow down and allow full engorgement before intercourse; replace the goal of intercourse with low-pressure erotic play dates; treat lack of knowledge as the source of fear and fear as the enemy of pleasure; keep desire alive with a deliberate balance of safety and novelty; and try skilled sex therapy before concluding a mismatch is fatal.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk] The advice she would give her 20-year-old self — "play full out, never play small, it is not your job to make other people feel comfortable" — doubles as her stance on sexual shame generally.[^orgasm-queen-do-this-for-20-minutes-before-havin-LLMCd3WbgGk]
