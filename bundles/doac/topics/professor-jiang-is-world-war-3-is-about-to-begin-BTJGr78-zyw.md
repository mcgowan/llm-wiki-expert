# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, Steven Bartlett interviews Professor Jiang, a geopolitics YouTuber who gained roughly 2.5 million subscribers in about a year, largely on the strength of predictions he made in 2024 — that Trump would win the election, start a war with Iran, and ultimately lose that war — the first two of which he says have come true.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw] The guest frames the US-Iran war as a forced move to save the petrodollar system, which he calls a Ponzi scheme: after the US sanctioned Russia in 2022, removed it from SWIFT, and had Europeans freeze over $200 billion in Russian assets, he argues the dollar lost its claim to political neutrality, pushing Russia, China, and Iran toward a gold-based Eurasian trading bloc that threatens American hegemony.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]

Using maps and a chess-set metaphor, the guest lays out the "grand strategies" of the US (fortress America plus global divide-and-rule, as he reads the published National Defense Strategy), Russia (the "Third Rome" project of uniting Eurasia), Israel (the "Greater Israel" project), and Iran (leadership of the Muslim world), and argues these competing visions make escalation to a global war 80-90% likely — indeed, he claims World War III has effectively already begun.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw] He explains why he believes decapitation strikes failed against mountainous Iran, why Iran's closure of the Strait of Hormuz and its decentralized "Mosaic" military structure make the war unwinnable quickly, and why Russia will eventually enter on Iran's side.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]

The episode closes with eight new predictions — including a Trump third term, a US national draft, an AI surveillance state, and East Asian conflict — followed by a philosophical turn in which the guest uses Plato's allegory of the cave to argue that private bankers and media construct perceived reality, and counsels listeners to respond to imperial collapse with community-building, creativity, and love rather than despair.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]

# Key points

- The guest says his three 2024 predictions — Trump winning in November 2024, Trump starting a war against Iran, and the US losing that war — were driven by the logic that America "had no choice" but to invade Iran to preserve its dollar-based empire.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He argues the 2022 response to Russia's invasion of Ukraine — sanctions, removal from SWIFT, and freezing over $200 billion in Russian assets — destroyed the political neutrality underpinning the dollar's reserve status and pushed Russia, China, and Iran toward a gold-based BRICS alternative.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- On why the US cannot win quickly: unlike flat, desert Iraq (conquered via "shock and awe" in about two weeks in 2003), Iran is a mountain fortress of 92 million people that can hide missiles underground and fight a war of attrition — a "game of uncle."[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He says Iran closed the Strait of Hormuz — only about 33 km wide, through which the GCC exports 20% of the world's energy — not by force but by making maritime insurance impossible, and now charges ships roughly $2 million tolls to cross; the GCC's dependence on importing 89% of its food and on vulnerable desalination plants compounds the crisis.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He reads the US National Defense Strategy (published by the Department of War) as a four-point imperial roadmap: control the Western Hemisphere (a "Don Monroe Doctrine" — hence Venezuela, Greenland, Canada, Cuba), make allies pay for their own wars, strangle China economically via the Strait of Malacca and First Island Chain, and supercharge the defense industrial base — noting the Pentagon has asked Ford and GM to consider switching from cars to munitions and drones.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He describes Iran's dual power structure — a pragmatic political leadership seeking settlement versus the IRGC, which sees a religious crusade against the "Great Satan" and runs a decentralized "Mosaic strategy" of 31 provincial armies that cannot be centrally stood down — making ceasefire negotiations "all for show."[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He assigns an 80-90% probability that the conflict becomes World War III, and argues it has already begun: the US is seizing Russian shadow-fleet tankers (citing January 2026 interceptions under Operation Southern Spear), which he calls acts of war, while signing choke-point agreements with Indonesia (Malacca) and Morocco (Gibraltar).[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He predicts Russia must enter the war on Iran's side — reinforcing Tehran via the Caspian Sea, financing the war with China through the Belt and Road, and extending its nuclear umbrella over Iran — because a conquered Iran would expose Russia's southern flank and sever both Russia's north-south corridor and China's Belt and Road.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- His most controversial new prediction is a Trump third term, via either Don Jr. running with Trump as vice president and then abdicating, or using emergency war powers to delay the 2028 election; he notes the Supreme Court, with three Trump-appointed justices, would decide the constitutional question.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- Further predictions: a US national draft with automatic registration of males aged 18-24 starting in December; a US-China "grand bargain" (China triangulating rather than fighting, with Trump and Xi meeting three or four times in the year); Israel achieving the Greater Israel project once America leaves the Middle East; NATO and Russia fighting over Odessa, which would give Russia control of a third of the world's grain exports; and East Asian conflict sparked by Taiwan, the Strait of Malacca, or North Korean extortion of a US-abandoned South Korea.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He also predicts an AI surveillance state built on digital ID and digital currency, describing China's existing system — where every online action feeds a database used to predict and steer behavior — as the model.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
- He believes the American empire will likely collapse within 5-10 years from debt, corruption, and inequality, drawing an analogy to the Bronze Age collapse and warning that fertilizer-dependent food systems could turn billions of hungry people into mass migration crises; via Plato's cave, he argues private bankers (the BIS, the Federal Reserve, Wall Street) construct the perceived reality that media — including Bartlett's own show, he tells the host — help enforce.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]

# Takeaways

The guest's overarching claim is that the world is already inside a slow-burning World War III, driven not by any single leader's irrationality but by the structural incentives of competing empires — and that the American empire, overstretched and over-indebted, is entering its twilight.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw] His prescribed response is personal rather than political: he tells listeners they cannot stop the wars or out-invest the collapse, but they can become community leaders — the neighbors who organize the apartment building during a blackout — and that creativity, kindness, and unconditional love (which he credits, through his wife, for his own transformation from depression to online success) are the real sources of resilience when empires and their narratives fall apart.[^professor-jiang-is-world-war-3-is-about-to-begin-BTJGr78-zyw]
