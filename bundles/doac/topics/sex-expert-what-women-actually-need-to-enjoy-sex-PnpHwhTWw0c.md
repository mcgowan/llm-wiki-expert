# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, host Steven Bartlett interviews Dr. Rena Malik, a urologist and widely followed sexual-health educator, about what actually drives good sex for both women and men. Malik organizes her advice around four "pillars" of sexual health: fuel (nutrition), strength (cardiovascular, resistance, and pelvic floor fitness), environment (stress, sleep, endocrine-disrupting chemicals, and social circle), and confidence (anatomical knowledge, curiosity, and the ability to talk about sex with a partner).[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]

A central thread of the conversation is Malik's claim that sexual function is a "canary in the coal mine" for overall health: erectile dysfunction often precedes cardiovascular disease by several years, sleep loss measurably tanks testosterone, and sexual frequency correlates with longevity in observational studies. She and Bartlett also discuss why young adults are having less sex — attention hijacked by phones, hookup culture, porn-derived scripts (including the normalization of choking), and chronic stress — and why arousal requires a calm, parasympathetic state and deliberately protected time and mental space.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]

The episode also covers female anatomy and pleasure in detail — the internal structure of the clitoris, the G-spot area and Skene's glands, squirting, erogenous zones, and positions and techniques that raise orgasm rates — alongside frank discussion of penis-size anxiety, testosterone replacement therapy, anabolic steroid risks, and how couples can learn to communicate about sex.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]

# Key points

- Malik frames sexual health around four pillars: fuel (diet), strength (cardio, resistance, and pelvic floor), environment (stress, sleep, chemicals, and social company), and confidence (knowledge and curiosity, including understanding female anatomy).[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- She cites longevity data: people who have sex about once a week showed 49% lower all-cause mortality than those having sex once a year, and a 1997 study found roughly 13% greater life expectancy per additional 100 orgasms per year in men — while acknowledging causation is hard to establish.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- On diet, she points to the Health Professionals Follow-up Study of about 20,000 men, in which Mediterranean-diet adherence was linked to a 22% lower risk of erectile dysfunction; she also cites studies on 100g of pistachios daily and regular blueberry intake improving erectile function, and adequate fiber (38g men / 25g women) supporting blood-vessel health.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- She claims 150 minutes a week of moderate cardiovascular exercise improves erectile function scores about as much as taking Viagra, and that even a supervised walking protocol in men with heart disease improved erectile function by 70%; maintaining muscle through resistance training makes men roughly three times less likely to have erectile dysfunction.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- Malik argues the pelvic floor is "under-evaluated": tight pelvic floor muscles can cause pain, urinary urgency, erectile dysfunction, and arousal problems, and Kegels can make things worse when muscles are already tense — relaxation work (diaphragmatic breathing, yoga stretches) or pelvic floor physical therapy is often what is needed.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- On sleep and hormones, she says a week of sleeping five hours a night drops men's testosterone about 15% — equivalent to roughly ten years of aging — and treating sleep apnea can raise testosterone by as much as 200 ng/dL; she also notes average testosterone levels fell about 25% between the late 1990s and 2015, blaming obesity, insulin resistance, ultra-processed food, and endocrine-disrupting chemicals like phthalates and BPA.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- She warns that erectile dysfunction affects about 52% of men over 50 (rising roughly 10% per decade) and often precedes heart disease by 3-5 years, making sexual problems an opportunity to investigate cardiovascular health; morning erections are a key sign that blood flow, nerves, and hormones are working.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- On female pleasure, Malik explains the clitoris is the homologue of the penis and extends internally far beyond the visible glans; she says only a minority of women orgasm from penetration alone, most need direct clitoral stimulation, woman-on-top positions and the coital alignment technique raise orgasm rates, and about 40% of women squirt (fluid from the bladder and Skene's glands that is chemically distinct from urine).[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- Citing Debby Herbenick's research, she notes choking has become normalized among college-age people — around 60% of women in that group report having been choked during sex — often performed because partners assume it is expected rather than because it is enjoyed.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- A small London study of 19 couples found slow caressing at about 3 cm/second (which activates C-tactile afferent nerve fibers that respond only to human touch) produced more arousal and pleasant sensation than faster stroking at 18 cm/second.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- On penis size, she says the erect average is about 5.3-5.5 inches, women's most-purchased sex toys are close to average size, and traction devices used 30 minutes twice daily can add about 2 cm over 3-6 months — while surgery is high-risk and "jelqing" can cause erectile dysfunction through micro-tears.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
- On TRT and steroids, she distinguishes testosterone replacement (restoring low levels in symptomatic men) from anabolic steroid abuse, which she says carries a 15x higher risk of premature heart failure and vastly higher cardiac-death risk; TRT suppresses fertility, with about 70% of men infertile after roughly 18 months of use, and testosterone below ~214 ng/dL is associated with doubled mortality risk.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]

# Takeaways

Malik's overarching message is that great sex is downstream of general health and deliberate attention: eat a Mediterranean-style diet, exercise, sleep, manage stress, mind the pelvic floor, and protect time and mental space for intimacy rather than treating sex as the last item on the schedule. She argues sexual problems should be treated as early warning signals worth investigating rather than sources of shame, that couples should learn to talk about sex outside the bedroom with curiosity rather than blame, and that pleasure — especially women's pleasure — depends far more on anatomical knowledge, communication, and presence than on penis size or performance.[^sex-expert-what-women-actually-need-to-enjoy-sex-PnpHwhTWw0c]
