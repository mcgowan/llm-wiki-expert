# Overview

Konstantin Kisin, born in the late Soviet Union and sent to a UK boarding school during a brief window of family wealth, tells Steven Bartlett that his outsider's vantage point is the whole basis of his commentary: people born into Western societies treat their freedoms as given, whereas he has seen that the world is not like this everywhere and that civilisations do not last forever.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] The privilege he says nobody discusses is Western privilege, and his book "An Immigrant's Love Letter to the West" is intended, he says, as an argument for gratitude toward private property, capitalism, the rule of law and free expression — contrasting them with what happens to a Khodorkovsky or a Jack Ma who crosses the wrong power.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]

His central thesis is that civilisations are destroyed by suicide rather than invasion, and that the ideology he calls wokeness performs that function by teaching people their own society is bad.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] He characterises it as the celebration of victimhood, arranged into a hierarchy of oppression that treats people as group members rather than individuals, and traces its intellectual lineage to a race-inflected reworking of Marxism adopted by Western academics once it became clear the working class would not revolt.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] He rejects fixed political labels for himself, arguing that ideology of any kind forces you to suspend your own moral judgement to stay in the tribe, and that left and right labels now function mainly as weapons.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]

Bartlett tests the argument repeatedly from his own experience — a business grant available to him as a black 18-year-old, name-based hiring discrimination, the role models who "killed his excuses" — and Kisin's answer each time is that whatever hand you are dealt, no cavalry is coming.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] The conversation then covers geopolitics, religion's decline, the position of young men, free speech, illegal immigration and climate policy, with Kisin returning throughout to a line he attributes to Thomas Sowell: there are no solutions, only trade-offs.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]

# Key points

- Kisin argues Western privilege is the one form of privilege never discussed, and that gratitude for it — freedom of speech, private property, the ability to build a life — is the antidote to what he sees as civilisational self-loathing.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He says the term "woke" began around 2014 as self-description among racial-justice activists awakened to intersectionality, and curdled into an insult as onlookers began mocking the behaviour it produced.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He contends the ideology asks the wrong question — why are people struggling, rather than what creates prosperity — and points to East Asian Americans being disadvantaged in college admissions as evidence that the oppression hierarchy penalises groups that succeed.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- On the psychological cost of victimhood he cites the scar experiment, in which participants told they had facial disfigurements — secretly removed before their interviews — still reported specific discriminatory comments about their faces, illustrating that perception is projection.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He treats stereotype threat as evidence of the power of being told the stereotype rather than the stereotype itself, and argues that well-meaning interventions are more dangerous than malicious ones because the benevolent tyrant is unrestrained by conscience.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He rejects "you can't be what you can't see" as one of the most pernicious recent ideas, and invokes Thomas Sowell's claim that over recent decades we have replaced things that work with things that sound good — with social media accelerating the shift because it lacks real-world feedback.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He points to police pullback in several US cities after George Floyd's killing as a case where an understandable emotional reaction produced action that harmed the community it was meant to protect.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- Geopolitically he names six great civilisations and argues that when the dominant one signals weakness, others make a play for the top spot — citing Ukraine, the Afghanistan withdrawal, Chinese posture toward Taiwan and the October 7 attack on Israel as consequences of perceived Western division.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He frames national debt above 100% of GDP as a broken intergenerational contract, and argues mass immigration is used to grow headline GDP while GDP per capita falls, leaving Gen Z poorer.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He cites Soviet defector Yuri Bezmenov on demoralisation — the claim that most Soviet intelligence effort went into dividing societies from within rather than stealing secrets — and the Tenet Media case, where he says roughly $10 million of Russia-linked money flowed to US influencers, arguing the aim is a cacophony of lies rather than a particular election result.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He says political correctness originated in the Soviet Union as a way of telling critics their claims might be factually correct but were politically inconvenient, and that its function has always been suppressing dissent.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- On speech he cites pandemic-era suppression of the lab-leak hypothesis and the Chelsea Russell case — a woman prosecuted for a hate crime after posting rap lyrics containing a slur in tribute to a friend killed in a car crash — and argues that Musk's purchase of Twitter mattered because censorship collapses once a single uncontrolled platform exists, while floating "freedom of speech but not freedom of reach" as a possible balance.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- On young men he argues Islam and combat sports both offer discipline, structure and permission to be masculine at a moment when strength and dominance are culturally suspect, and describes Andrew Tate as a symptom rather than the cause, contrasting him with Jordan Peterson.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He says men are evolutionarily disposable in a way that means nobody feels sorry for them, so victimhood is especially corrosive for men, and insists the goal is not sex-based grievance but men and women working together and filling each other's gaps.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- On immigration he distinguishes legal from illegal sharply, opposes demonising migrants themselves, describes UK spending he puts at £8 million a day housing illegal arrivals, condemns hotel arson as moronic, and proposes offshore refugee processing centres in conflict regions as the practical trade-off.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- His prescription for saving the West is domestic and small-scale: take your children abroad to see how others live, teach them where their prosperity came from, and inoculate them against ideas he says are false — the same thing, he notes, his parents did for him before his first day at a Soviet school.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
- He calls his final unaddressed theme "trade-off denialism", citing Hungary halving abortions through family incentives rather than bans, and arguing on climate that Indian life expectancy rose from 32 in 1947 to about 71 on the back of fossil fuels, that net zero exports emissions, and that the answer is technological — chiefly far more nuclear power.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]

# Takeaways

Kisin's argument holds together around a single move: he treats cultural questions as questions about incentives and consequences rather than intentions, and judges policies by whether they produce the outcome they promise.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] He is candid that this leaves him in the centre, which he calls the place of greatest tension because fire comes from both sides, and he credits the internet for making a nuanced audience economically viable at all.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q] Both men end by agreeing that the same conversation conducted in bad faith would have been impossible — a point Kisin offers as his own working principle rather than a claim about anyone else.[^the-anti-woke-expert-we-are-witnessing-the-fall-GbV6iW26h-Q]
