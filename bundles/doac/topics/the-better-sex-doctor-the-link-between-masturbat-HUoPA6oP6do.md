# Overview

Dr. Rena Malik, a board-certified urologist with a fellowship in female pelvic medicine, tells Steven Bartlett that her mission is simple and largely unmet: to establish that sexual health is health, and to make accurate information about it freely available.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] She argues that almost nobody understands their own body in this domain, partly because doctors do not ask — a primary care physician might raise erections, she says, but will not ask a woman whether she orgasms or anyone whether sex is pleasurable — and partly because the script people absorb from television and pornography bears no resemblance to real sex.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] The consequence, in her account, is a population walking around with private shame about entirely normal physiology, from nocturnal erections to needing more foreplay.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]

A recurring theme is the pelvic floor — the bowl of muscles supporting the bladder, rectum and, in women, the vagina and uterus — which Malik says most people, and nearly all men, do not know they have.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] She links both weakness and excessive tone to a wide range of problems including urinary urgency, constipation, pain, and erectile difficulty, and notes she saw a rise in pelvic floor dysfunction clinically during COVID as people sat all day.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] She is also emphatic that erectile dysfunction can be a cardiovascular warning sign, because penile arteries are narrower than coronary ones and clog sooner.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]

Much of the episode is myth-clearing: on how often couples actually have sex, how long sex actually lasts, whether masturbation lowers testosterone, whether abstinence improves athletic performance, and what does and does not change penis and vagina size.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] Underneath the specifics, Malik's repeated prescription is communication — knowing what you like and being able to say so — which she notes nobody was ever taught how to do.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]

# Key points

- Malik cites research finding that men who ejaculate 21 or more times a month are less likely to develop prostate cancer, attributed to a "prostate stagnation" hypothesis, while cautioning that the frequency is a statistical threshold rather than a target and that healthier men may simply be more sexually active.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- She says partnered couples average about once a week, calls comparison to a benchmark actively harmful, and argues quality matters more than quantity.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Stopwatch studies across roughly 15,000 people put average intercourse from penetration to male climax at 5.1 to 5.7 minutes, with national variation (around 4 minutes in Turkey, around 10 in the UK), while women want it to last 18 to 25 minutes and men around 12 to 16.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Average time to orgasm for women is about 14 minutes, and 85% of women need some form of clitoral stimulation to climax; Malik notes that on a first sexual encounter women orgasm about 45% of the time with men and 95% of the time with women, which she reads as an education gap rather than a defect.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Around 15% of men diagnosed with erectile dysfunction have a heart attack within seven years, and roughly 50% of men who have a heart attack had erectile dysfunction beforehand — because penile arteries are 1-2 mm versus 3-4 mm coronary arteries, so occlusion shows up there first.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- She distinguishes spontaneous from responsive desire, noting men more often report the former and women the latter especially in long relationships, and recommends scheduling intimacy rather than sex so the calendar entry does not carry performance pressure.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- On testosterone she says the widely cited claim that abstinence raises it rests on a single 10-man, 21-day study showing a roughly 50 ng/dL rise, which she considers small and confounded by anticipation; her evidence-backed levers are instead sleep (under five to six hours costs 10-15%), resistance training of large lower-body muscle groups, and adequate healthy fats, while chronic endurance training can raise cortisol and suppress testosterone.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Average sperm count has fallen roughly 50% over the last 50 years, which Malik attributes to increasing sedentariness and metabolic disease plus probable environmental contributors such as microplastics and endocrine-disrupting chemicals; she advises avoiding plastic bottles and reheating food in glass as controllable steps.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Heat genuinely matters for sperm production — phones in the lap, laptops on the lap, saunas, hot tubs and even a fever can temporarily suppress it — and she says the mechanism is temperature rather than Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- She considers masturbation safe unless it displaces work, partners or sleep, but warns that always masturbating the same way habituates the body and can make climax with a partner harder; on No Nut November she has no objection to the challenge, only to the shame, noting 86% of men have had a nocturnal emission, which is physiological and not a failure.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Studies of athletic performance have not found that abstaining from ejaculation improves it, and she notes disrupting an established routine can itself hurt performance.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Orgasm involuntarily contracts the pelvic floor roughly every 0.8 seconds for 5 to 60 seconds, which functions as pelvic floor exercise — so, contrary to the myth, frequent sex likely strengthens rather than loosens a woman's pelvic floor; weakness comes from childbirth, collagen disorders and prolonged standing.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- On Kegels she stresses the relaxation phase as much as the squeeze, recommends starting lying down, and warns that if the pelvic floor is already too tight, more clenching causes harm; a pelvic floor physical therapist is the equivalent of a personal trainer for it.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- On penis size she says traction devices are the safest evidence-backed lengthening method (original protocols required 6-8 hours daily for months for about 2 cm), pumps do not add length, men estimate the ideal at about 6 inches versus an average of 5.1 to 5.3, and a Japanese study found nose length rather than hand or foot length correlated with penile length.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- She notes the research imbalance directly — searching "penis" returns around 50,000 articles against roughly 2,000 for "clitoris" — and says squirting remains genuinely unresolved after only three or four studies, with analyses finding dilute urine and prostate-specific antigen but no conclusive account of the fluid's origin.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
- Malik says the average age at which a boy first sees pornography is 13, with exposure as young as 8 to 10, and that problematic use is put at about 4% in the literature; she does not consider porn inherently bad for adults but worries about children forming their sexual template from it, and about VR making avoidance of real intimacy easier still.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]

# Takeaways

Malik's answer to almost every dysfunction is the same two-part move: rule out the physiological cause properly, then address the psychological component, which she says is present in everyone with sexual dysfunction because feeling abnormal in an intimate space is inherently distressing.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] She is insistent that conversations about sex should happen outside the bedroom, in I-statements, repeatedly rather than once, and with professional help where it is available.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do] Her closing message is that sexual health is health — that its failures reach into mood, productivity and relationships — and that better education for young people is the highest-leverage fix; she adds, as advice to the students she mentors, that the single most consequential decision is who you choose as a partner.[^the-better-sex-doctor-the-link-between-masturbat-HUoPA6oP6do]
