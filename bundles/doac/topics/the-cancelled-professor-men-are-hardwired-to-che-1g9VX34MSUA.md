# Overview

Gad Saad, an evolutionary behavioural scientist who founded the application of evolutionary theory to consumer psychology, tells Steven Bartlett that he has devoted his career to the pursuit of truth and the defence of freedoms, and that no research topic should be off limits provided it is pursued honestly under the scientific method.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] He describes his own intellectual turning point as reading Martin Daly and Margo Wilson's "Homicide" as a doctoral student at Cornell, which applied a Darwinian lens to patterns of criminality that recur across cultures and eras.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] Throughout the conversation he repeatedly insists that explaining a behaviour evolutionarily is not endorsing it — the oncologist studying cancer is not pro-cancer — while acknowledging that many of his academic colleagues are precisely the people who cannot hold that distinction.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]

Much of the discussion covers mating. Saad argues that both sexes evolved a desire for sexual variety, men more strongly, and that monogamy is therefore an institutional arrangement rather than a natural default.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] He develops the idea of a mate desirability score — treating a person, like a car, as a bundle of attributes — and argues that people assort on that overall score, hypothesising that divergence in partners' scores over time predicts divorce.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] Crucially, he frames mating as compensatory: since people are judged on a basket of goods rather than a single trait, almost anyone can raise their score through fitness, reading, vocabulary, assertiveness and ambition.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]

The second half moves to Saad's public commentary. He explains the parasitic-mind metaphor he took from neuroparasitology, argues that postmodernism is the ancestor of the ideas he considers parasitic, and defends a deontological reading of free speech, presumption of innocence and journalistic integrity against what he calls consequentialist intrusions.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] He recounts his Jewish childhood in Lebanon, his family's flight to Montreal and his parents' 1980 kidnapping, and previews a forthcoming book on what he terms suicidal empathy.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] Bartlett pushes back repeatedly — on the harms of speech, on trans people caught in a raging public argument, and on Donald Trump's character — and Saad concedes that Trump likely fails his own standard of personal conduct.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]

# Key points

- Saad says the strongest predictor of child abuse in a home is the presence of a stepparent, describing the effect as roughly hundredfold rather than the 1.2 odds ratios typical of published science, and links it to systematic infanticide by incoming male lions taking over a pride.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He argues the most dangerous person a woman will ever meet, across tribes and eras, is her husband, with suspected or discovered infidelity the overwhelming trigger — a pattern he attributes to human males being a biparental species facing paternity uncertainty, for which there is no maternal equivalent.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He maintains that explaining such behaviours does not justify them, since humans also evolved self-control and a moral compass, and that navigating competing Darwinian pulls is the difficulty of life.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- On female sexual variety he cites three lines of evidence: women are most likely to stray when maximally fertile and less likely to insist on contraception then; comparative primate testes size scales with female promiscuity (small in mountain gorillas, enormous in chimpanzees); and Robin Baker's "Sperm Wars" claim of blocker and killer sperm phenotypes, which he says implies ancestral sperm competition within a 72-hour window.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He distinguishes polygyny from polyandry, noting that roughly 85% of documented cultures institutionalise monogamy and that near-absent polyandry survives mainly as Tibetan fraternal polyandry, explicable through inclusive fitness.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- His own research on gift-giving found investment tracking the coefficient of genetic relatedness, and a study of about 30 Israeli weddings found the maternal side gave larger gifts than the paternal side — consistent with the prediction that the maternal grandmother, facing no generational paternity uncertainty, invests most and the paternal grandfather least.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He organises consumer behaviour into four Darwinian modules — survival, reproductive, kin selection and reciprocal altruism — using Ferrari ownership (which he says is about 99% male) as a peacock's-tail sexual signal, and birthday dinners as the reciprocity ritual that oils social bonds.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He claims the number one universal attribute women seek is anything signalling social status — ambition, assertiveness, dominance, accomplishment — noting the guitar-in-a-personal-ad effect and distinguishing proximate from ultimate explanations of why it works.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He describes assortative mating as birds of a feather for long-term pairings and opposites attract only for short-term ones, and offers as an untested hypothesis that divorce follows when partners' mate values diverge over time.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He cites a study of 720 couples in which only one violated the norm of the man being taller, and argues that being older, tall and highly educated compounds difficulty for women in the mating market because high-status women insist more, not less, on a partner at or above their status.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- On masculinity he argues that pathologising male approach behaviour — mandatory training modules, the label toxic masculinity, treating compliments as violations — deters young men who lack a strong sense of personhood, illustrating boldness with a story about his short, judo-Olympian brother.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- Shown an ITV undercover clip of a woman approached 20 times in two hours in Cardiff, Saad answers with Aristotle's golden mean and the inverted U, arguing those men are at the reckless extreme and that an empathetic person would recognise her vulnerability — and links most such social violations to failures of self-awareness and theory of mind.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He frames pornography not as an adaptation but an exaptation that piggybacks on evolved male preferences for visual stimuli and sexual variety, placing it alongside gambling, eating disorders and compulsive buying as dark-side consumption, and comparing addiction to the hyperactive warning-flag loop of OCD.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- On happiness he says roughly 50% of individual variation is genetic, leaving half to choices and mindsets, and names choice of spouse and choice of profession as the two decisions that dominate — recommending temporal freedom over what he calls scheduling asphyxia and jobs that allow a creative impulse.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He explains Frank Sulloway's "Born to Rebel" birth-order thesis — 23 of 28 radical scientific innovations coming from laterborns — via Darwinian niche partitioning, and says he replicated the framework showing lastborns are more likely to be product innovators and early adopters.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He describes toxoplasma-style neuroparasites as the model for his "parasitic mind" thesis, names postmodernism as the granddaddy of parasitic ideas, and calls himself a free-speech absolutist whose only exception is direct incitement to violence — a position he stresses he holds as a Jew who supports even Holocaust deniers' right to speak.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- He rejects the concept of forbidden knowledge, citing Philippe Rushton's research on racial differences as a case where he says honestly collected data should still be published, and argues that bad claims should be defeated with better evidence rather than suppressed.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
- His forthcoming "suicidal empathy" argument holds that empathy misfires when hyperactive or aimed at the wrong target, applied to immigration, repeat offenders and San Francisco retail theft; he says he would vote for Trump over Kamala Harris while conceding Trump probably lacks the self-control and moral character he personally values.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]

# Takeaways

Saad's recurring move is to separate description from prescription — insisting that identifying an evolved tendency, whether toward infidelity, status-seeking or in-group thinking, tells you what you are working against rather than what you should do.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] The practical corollary he offers is optimistic: because mate value is compensatory and happiness is only half heritable, most of the levers he discusses are ones he says an individual can actually pull.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA] Asked what remark has stayed with him, he cites his mother's warning that the world does not abide by his purity bubble — a standard he admits leaves him routinely disappointed in people.[^the-cancelled-professor-men-are-hardwired-to-che-1g9VX34MSUA]
