# Overview

Dr Mindy Pelz returns to the podcast to argue that health has been over-outsourced — to doctors, pills and now to GLP-1 drugs — and that fasting is valuable partly because it hands control back to the individual.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] She opens with listener comments about weight loss and restored confidence, and says what moves her most is not the pounds but the sense of capability people report; in her framing, "only you can close your mouth and not eat," so any result belongs entirely to the person who achieved it.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] Her physiological case rests on metabolic switching: after roughly 8 to 12 hours without food, glucose falls and the body shifts to burning fat and producing ketones, which she says sharpens focus, brings up the calming neurotransmitter GABA, lowers inflammation and switches off hunger.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]

On Ozempic, Pelz is careful to say she is not an expert on its mechanisms and does not want to villainise a drug that can be a lifeline in a metabolic crisis, but raises cost, side effects, discontinuation rates and the absence of long-term data — particularly in women.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] Her deeper objection is that it treats a symptom while leaving the cause untouched: she argues obesogenic chemicals permitted in food are reprogramming stem cells into fat cells, and that this, not simple lack of discipline, explains much of the obesity crisis in children and adults.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] She calls the failure to fix the food system criminal, and attributes it to the money at stake in the industry.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]

A large part of the episode addresses women specifically, following her book *Fast Like a Girl* and its follow-up *Eat Like a Girl*.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] She argues estrogen and progesterone respond to fasting in opposite ways and that women should not fast in the week before their period, when progesterone needs higher glucose and lower cortisol.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] She closes with a "toolbox" metaphor, unpacking a physical toolbox with Bartlett to argue that supplements, strength training, cardio, fasting, protein, fats, fibre and human connection are all tools whose value is context-dependent, and that the rigidity of health debates about which single tool is best is itself the mistake.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]

# Key points

- Pelz says the fundamental lie is that all food is safe, pointing to the US "generally recognized as safe" (GRAS) category, which she says lets chemicals into food without proof of safety under an innocent-until-proven-guilty philosophy, and jokes that within the FDA people say the "F" is silent.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She traces "breakfast is the most important meal of the day" to a 1970s Kellogg's Corn Flakes advertising slogan, and says she can find no research supporting the idea that eating more frequently speeds metabolism.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She distinguishes time-restricted eating — which triggers healing mechanisms through nutrient absence and falling glucose — from calorie restriction, crediting researchers such as Valter Longo and Satchin Panda with establishing the case for compressed eating windows.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- On Ozempic she cites roughly $800 a month in cost, debated side effects such as nausea, and a statistic that around 70% of users are off the drug within two years, which she reads as evidence it is not a strong long-term solution.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She frames fasting as achieving the same hunger suppression by different means — the body burns its own fat to make a ketone, which then shuts off the hunger hormone — and says that unlike a drug it leaves people feeling they did it themselves.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She describes an "every other day diet" study in which metabolically unwell participants alternated eating freely with full-day fasts for a year; about a third dropped out, but those who stayed improved every metabolic marker, lost weight, and — most surprising to the researchers — found their cravings changed at around six months, which she attributes to shifts in the gut microbiome.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- Answering the criticism that fasting destroys gut bacteria, she cites MIT research on a 24-hour fast generating intestinal stem cells that repair the gut, and argues the first meal after a fast matters — her own go-to being avocado with sauerkraut and hemp seeds to feed beneficial bacteria.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She names processed meats (citing a study linking regular consumption to higher leukemia rates in children), sugar and sugary cereals, and the toxic oils in items such as donuts, juice boxes and tube yogurts as cancer-feeding foods, and calls "all natural" an unregulated buzzword.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She defines obesogens as food chemicals that reprogram stem cells — particularly those that would become bone cells — into fat cells, and links this to children today being shorter and heavier than ever, citing a review from Center/Citizen Science for Public Interest.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She cites obesity figures of roughly 73% of Americans overweight or obese, around 64% in England, plus about 30% of children under 13 and 50% of teenagers.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She recommends hemoglobin A1c as a personal metric, targeting around 5, explaining that higher values mean slower glucose clearance and glycation, in which excess glucose gums up red blood cells and impairs oxygen delivery to tissue.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- On longer fasts she cites research that around 36 hours in the body begins burning belly fat specifically, that systemic stem cell production picks up at about 72 hours, and recounts resolving a persistent Achilles injury with a five-day water fast — while conceding she has seen no musculoskeletal-repair research and offering to look for it.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She describes Longo's fast-mimicking diet — under 800 calories and under 20g of protein for five days a month over three months — as having shown pancreatic cell regrowth in type 1 diabetics.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- On women's cycles she advises against fasting in the week before menstruation, warning that repeatedly pushing through can suppress the progesterone peak and cost women their cycles — and notes menstrual blood carries phthalates, parabens, forever chemicals and plastics, so not bleeding means not detoxing.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She proposes three no-questions-asked days off per month for all employees, with education encouraging women to take them the week before their period, and endorses Bartlett's existing unlimited-holiday policy as achieving the same without forcing disclosure.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- She describes a "hormonal hierarchy" with sex hormones at the base, insulin above them and cortisol above that, and calls oxytocin the most powerful hormone on the planet — recommending connection, gratitude, walks, petting a dog or "havening" to lower cortisol, and citing a study she heard of in which lonely smokers had higher lung cancer incidence than smokers with strong relationships.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
- Asked for her policy wish list, she names universal body and food education, continuous glucose monitors for every adult so people can see their own responses, and an outright ban on chemicals in food — acknowledging that a grocery store where everything spoils within a week is bad for business, which is why it does not exist.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]

# Takeaways

Pelz's through-line is that health is a personal responsibility that cannot be delegated, and that the payoff for prevention is invisible by definition — "prevention doesn't come with a ribbon."[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA] Rather than any single protocol, she advocates variation and self-experimentation: feast-famine cycling rather than rigid one-meal-a-day rules, protein dosed across the day rather than in one sitting, eating in the light rather than the dark, and building a personal toolbox whose contents change with what the body needs that day.[^the-fasting-expert-the-truth-about-ozempic-these-MrjIXLQ_OdA]
