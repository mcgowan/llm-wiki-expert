# Overview

In this return appearance on The Diary Of A CEO, board-certified trial attorney and communication author Jefferson Fisher tells Steven Bartlett that handling conflict is a learned discipline, not a natural gift — a set of skills he distilled from preparing terrified clients for cross-examination and depositions.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8] His core stance is that "being right is overrated": the goal of an argument is not to win but to stay in control, because an emotional outburst signals you are out of your depth and costs you credibility, while the calm person becomes the "anchor" everyone in the room trusts — the way jurors read an attorney's unbothered reaction to an adverse ruling, or passengers read a calm flight attendant during turbulence.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]

Fisher distinguishes gaslighting from lying — lying is a surface falsehood, gaslighting is intentionally altering another person's reality for control — and insists everyone has gaslit at some point as self-preservation.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8] He argues the people most susceptible are anxiously attached "co-regulators," says in his experience it is overwhelmingly women who report being gaslit (no man has ever come to him saying so), and prescribes standing still in the truth — "I remember that differently" — rather than chasing the holes a gaslighter or narcissist digs.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]

Much of the conversation turns to relationships: Fisher's claim that "most relationships don't fall apart because they fell out of love — they fell out of communication," his emphasis on rapid repair and validation over dismissal, and his closing five principles of masterful communication — authenticity, reducing distraction, not over-explaining, knowing how to meet others' grief, and knowing how to handle insults and manipulators.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]

# Key points

- The guest frames communication as an investment with a bill that always comes due: every time you don't say what needs to be said — at work, in a relationship — you pay in lost promotions, lost worth, and carried resentment, sometimes for decades.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- His courtroom presence ritual is to arrive early, touch the chairs and banisters, and tell himself "this is my living room, and everybody else is just visiting"; he limits himself to essentially one objection per trial because constant objecting signals to jurors that you are hiding evidence.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- Gaslighting, per Fisher, is distinguished from lying by intent: the gaslighter wants to be "director, producer, and actor" of your reality for control — and the defense is to slow the conversation down and repeat a fixed truth ("I remember that differently") instead of scrambling to plug the holes they dig.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- He says people who misuse the term — calling any unwelcome criticism "gaslighting" or hiding behind "that's just my boundary" — are themselves performing a form of self-preservation, and notes wryly that nobody ever describes their own ex as anything but "my narcissistic ex."[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- For narcissists, whose game he describes as "praise or provoke," his playbook is: limit exposure, refuse to play, and use neutral statements ("Noted," "Good to know," "Thanks for sharing") they cannot grab onto; hallmarks include never being happy for anyone else, a victim mentality, lack of empathy, and obsession with how the crowd perceives them — which he exploits in depositions by asking expert witnesses "And you think the jury's going to like that?"[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- His line between insecurity and narcissism: not all insecure people are narcissists, but all narcissists are insecure — the difference being that insecure people still seek growth while narcissists believe they have nothing left to learn.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- To handle insults, he teaches a three-step sequence: 5–7 seconds of uncomfortable silence, ask them to repeat it ("I need you to say that again"), then ask "Did you mean for that to sound rude?" — which the host connects to cognitive dissonance, since almost nobody can affirm they intended harm.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- On couples' conflict, Fisher argues relationships fall apart through "a hundred moments where repair could have happened and it didn't"; his tools are validate first ("frustration comes next — if I respond first with frustration, I'm going to lose every time"), ask for resets ("Can I try that again?" — nobody has ever refused), slice issues thinner, and take timeouts, citing the Brené Brown clip about telling your partner what percentage you have "in the tank" so marriage is never falsely assumed to be 50/50.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- He cites a therapy maxim — "if it's hysterical, it's historical" — illustrated by his own spender-versus-saver marriage conflict, which traced back to childhood hand-me-downs that made buying "the nice thing" a proxy for self-worth.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- On grief, his rule is never to open with "Let me know if you need anything" — that hands the grieving person a chore; instead do the thing (bring the food, mow the yard), validate the unfairness specifically, and if you say you're praying for someone, actually text them the prayer.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- His fakeness detectors are "bestie bombing" (instant best-friendship, which secure people never do), over-complimenting, and showing no genuine interest in you; conversely he holds that "presence is the highest form of authenticity," demonstrated with a taut string exercise showing how pulling out a phone slackens the connection — even a face-down phone on the table signals divided priority.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
- He distinguishes nice from kind — nice is surface people-pleasing, kind (from "kin") cares enough to tell the truth — and cites a recent study that the biggest predictor of a child's well-being is not whether parents were married or divorced but how they handled conflict.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]

# Takeaways

Fisher's closing summary names five pillars of masterful communication — authenticity, reducing distraction, not over-explaining ("be a well, not a waterfall"; let your first word be your breath), knowing how to be present with people's sadness, and knowing how to handle insults, narcissists, and gaslighters — all in service of what he calls the last domino people are really after: self-worth.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8] The guest's overarching claim is that the quality of a relationship equals the quality of its communication: conflict is not the enemy but the mechanism of growth, provided each cut heals to 101% rather than 99%.[^the-gaslighting-expert-jefferson-fisher-if-they-ZPVdK9v0CK8]
