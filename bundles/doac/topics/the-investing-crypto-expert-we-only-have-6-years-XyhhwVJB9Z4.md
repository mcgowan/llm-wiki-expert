# Overview

Steven Bartlett interviews Raoul Pal, a former hedge fund manager and author of the research service Global Macro Investor who founded Real Vision after the 2008 and 2012 crises convinced him that ordinary people were being kept in the dark about how the financial system works.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] He describes watching friends in Spain get wiped out when European banks were "bailed in" — depositors' savings used to pay debtors — and says the recurring question "why didn't we know?" became the mission behind his media and education businesses, which he summarises with the phrase "unfuck your future."[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]

Pal's central macro claim is what he calls "the everything code": since 2008 the developed world has been trapped in a debt cycle in which governments must print money roughly every four years to service obligations, and the result is a debasement of currency running at about 8% a year on top of roughly 3% inflation — a hurdle rate of about 11% that he argues makes the S&P 500 (roughly 10–11% a year) a break-even proposition, real estate marginal, and gold a loser in real terms.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] Measured against that hurdle, he says only two asset classes have beaten it: technology stocks (the Nasdaq at about 18% a year) and crypto (Bitcoin at about 145–150% a year since 2011), which is why he says 100% of his liquid net worth has been in crypto for five years.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]

The second half of the conversation is a plain-language explanation of banking, blockchain and risk. Using a suitcase as a bank and a physical chain as a blockchain, Pal walks through fractional reserve banking, rehypothecated collateral, the Byzantine Generals problem, consensus, smart contracts and digital scarcity, arguing that crypto is less about money than about "a source of truth" and that it is the first global infrastructure ordinary people can own before institutions do.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] Alongside this he lays out a career playbook — become an expert then a generalist, apply your expertise to price-insensitive customers, ride a secular trend — and a warning about AI, which he calls the greatest innovation in human history and expects to break the economic model within a few years.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]

# Key points

- Pal argues that a 30-year-old today is structurally worse off than one in 1983, citing figures for living independently (85% down to 64%), marriage among 30-year-olds (80% to 47%), having children (60% to 32%) and home ownership (50% to 32%), and calls people now in their 30s the first generation that will not be as rich as their parents.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He says he bought a London house at 3.5 times his salary at 30, while the same property today would be 8–10 times an equivalent banker's salary, and argues that a house is a "lifestyle bank" — a source of security and quality of life — rather than a wealth-creation vehicle.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- On debasement, he argues that money loses about 8% of its purchasing power a year against scarce assets, and that adding roughly 3% inflation means "your future self is getting poorer by 11% every year" — so an S&P 500 return of about 11% leaves an investor no better off for the risk taken.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He puts US debt at roughly 380% of GDP and world debt at about 400%, argues that governments must therefore inject liquidity on a roughly four-year cycle tied to the 2008 rate reset, and says letting the debt collapse would mean something like a 75% wipeout of every asset.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- Comparing long-run returns he cites the S&P at 10–11% a year, the Nasdaq at about 18%, Bitcoin at about 145% a year since 2011 despite three 80% drawdowns, Ethereum at about 160% a year since 2015, and Solana at about 250% — and says the Nasdaq is down 99.97% priced in Bitcoin since 2012.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- His forecast is that crypto grows from roughly $2 trillion to $100 trillion by around 2032–2034; he refuses to give a price prediction because he says it creates false comfort, but Bartlett has AI compute the implied numbers live — roughly $2.1–2.6 million per Bitcoin at 40–50% dominance, and about $125,000 per Ethereum at 15% share, against then-current prices of $62,000 and $2,400.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- His allocation advice is to put 80–90% into Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana, invest a fixed amount monthly regardless of price, use no leverage, and reserve at most 10% for speculation that will most likely go to zero; the single instruction he repeats is "your one job is not to lose your tokens."[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He explains fractional reserve banking with a suitcase — the bank lends out your coin, you become a creditor rather than an owner, and protection typically stops around 100,000 in local currency — and notes that in 2008 the safest asset, US government bonds, was leveraged up to 30 times, meaning 30 parties believed they owned the same collateral.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- Blockchain, in his framing, solves the Byzantine Generals problem by replacing a bribable trusted third party with consensus among tens of thousands of computers, and smart contracts extend that to everything humans do — because, as he puts it, money, government, religion and concert tickets are all contracts.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He calls AI "the single greatest innovation of humanity ever," second only to splitting the atom, arguing it destroys the scarcity of knowledge that underpins white-collar pay, that agentic AI capable of building whole online businesses was about six months away, and that society will split between people who merge with machines and people who reject them.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- On investing in AI he is pessimistic for retail investors: a $2–3 trillion Microsoft going to $10–15 trillion is only a 5x, and the real returns accrue to VCs and founders, which he says is why crypto matters — it is fractionalisable, globally homogeneous and available to a Nigerian or a Brazilian on the same terms as a Silicon Valley insider.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- His career advice is to work relentlessly through your 20s to mid-30s, become an expert in one thing and a generalist in many, "manifest your destiny" by envisaging yourself five years out and reverse-engineering the steps, and then apply that expertise to price-insensitive buyers — cleaning Google's windows rather than Dorothy's bungalow in Plymouth.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He argues the counter-trend to digitisation is equally investable — nature, experiences, guiding and in-person community — noting his own three weeks off-roading in Zambia, a record Cayman tourist season in 2022 despite economic pain, and Character AI's hundreds of millions of conversations as evidence that loneliness is the market being underserved.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
- He is candid about his own errors: he first wrote a Bitcoin strategy piece around 2012–13 at $200 predicting $1 million, then sold at roughly 10–12x in 2017 before the run to $20,000, and says simply holding would have made five times more, while buying every four-year drawdown would have made about 25 times more.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]

# Takeaways

Pal's argument is that the rules of wealth creation have changed underneath most people's feet: because money is being debased faster than conventional savings compound, he contends that the traditional path of a mortgage, a pension and an index fund no longer produces a richer future self, and that the compensating opportunity — for now — is exposure to technology and crypto held patiently through 70–80% drawdowns.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] He frames the six-year window as the runway before AI and blockchain adoption reprice everything, and insists the biggest risk to individuals is not being wrong about the trend but self-sabotage through leverage, meme-coin gambling and selling early.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] He closes by relativising the whole thesis: money is "just a scoring system that allows you privileges," and the trade he says he will make until he dies is quality of life and experiences.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4] Listeners should note that Pal has direct commercial and financial interests in the assets he recommends — he runs Real Vision and the crypto fund Exponential Age Asset Management, sits on the Sui foundation board, and states that 100% of his liquid net worth is in crypto — and that Bartlett discloses owning Ethereum and running the Web3 company Thirdweb.[^the-investing-crypto-expert-we-only-have-6-years-XyhhwVJB9Z4]
