# Overview

Four weeks after his first appearance, University of Chicago professor Robert Pape returns to The Diary Of A CEO to assess the US–Iran war 40 days in. Pape — who taught conventional targeting for the US Air Force and says he modeled a hypothetical bombing campaign of Iran in his classes for 21 years — claims the war has unfolded almost exactly as his simulations predicted: US bombers destroy the industrial enrichment facilities but cannot destroy the enriched uranium itself, which Iran dispersed in advance. His core assessment is stark: "We're not weakening Iran. We have strengthened Iran," because Iran has deeply buried its drone and missile arsenals, and America cannot stop the drone attacks closing the Strait of Hormuz.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]

Pape introduces a "stage four" to his escalation-trap framework: beyond bombing, regime decapitation, and ground preparations lies Iran's emergence as a fourth center of world power alongside the US, China, and Russia. He argues Iran's selective blockade of Hormuz — through which 80–90% of transiting oil flows to Asia — is converting military leverage into geopolitical power, pulling India, Japan, Iraq, and Oman away from Washington while Saudi Arabia turns to Pakistan for security guarantees. He predicts the war will oscillate between stage three (ground-war preparations) and stage four for months, and maintains a 70% probability that a US ground operation begins.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]

The conversation also dwells on the human and political costs: Pape calls Trump's threat to end Iran's "civilization" the most declared statement of genocidal intent ever made by an American president, argues it is bonding Iran's pro-democracy movement to the regime, and closes by urging voters in democracies to break the "legitimacy shock cycle" by consistently backing centrist candidates.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]

# Key points

- Pape says his 21 years of classroom modeling — including a session three weeks before Operation Midnight Hammer that matched the actual platforms used, down to the B-2s — consistently found the same result: you can destroy the enrichment facilities but never the enriched material, like destroying the pan while the gold survives.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- He draws a direct analogy to Vietnam's Ho Chi Minh Trail, where the US cut throughput by over 80% yet the remaining fraction sustained the Viet Cong and boosted their morale; likewise, he argues, the US has hit 11,000–12,000 above-ground targets in Iran but cannot reach the buried final 10–20% of drones and missiles.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- Pape asserts Iran's leadership is intact and functional under the new supreme leader — decentralized to avoid targeting, not chaotic — and that decision-making in the White House is "far more chaotic" than in Tehran, which he reads as evidence Trump is losing power.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- He describes Israel as a three-time "diplomatic spoiler": killing Iranian negotiators during the June 12-day war, starting the February 28 bombing that killed the supreme leader and dozens of "doves," and assassinating Ali Larijani — the contact for a 10-point peace proposal Trump had called workable — on March 17, 2026.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- The US-led Gulf coalition is fragmenting into three pools, per Pape: Iraq distancing itself from US presence, Oman being drawn into revenue-sharing with Iran, and Saudi Arabia/UAE seeking protection from nuclear-armed Pakistan — because US bases proved to be "big fat targets" and Trump declined to defend them.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- He argues a US ground option would begin with Marine amphibious beachheads (roughly 100 by 20 miles) on Iran's Hormuz coast or in the southwestern oil fields, notes Trump has repeatedly said "if it were up to me, I would take the oil," and warns American casualties would produce "finish the job" politics rather than withdrawal — locking in a six-month ground war minimum.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- Pape calls Trump's Truth Social threat that "a whole civilization will die tonight" unprecedented — noting 500 Minuteman III missiles with 100–300 kiloton warheads can be retargeted in 45 minutes — and contrasts it with Truman, who framed Hiroshima as destroying Japan's military power, not its civilization.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- He warns that destroying Iran's electric grid (about 130 nodes, with the top 10 sufficient to collapse the network) would knock out generation for 6–18 months if the turbine halls were hit, ending dialysis, surgeries, and food refrigeration and measurably lowering the life expectancy of Iran's 92 million people.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- On the world balance of power, Pape argues Iran now controls 20% of the world's oil and Russia 11%; acting together with China they could pull roughly 30% of world oil off the market, driving US inflation and bond rates on a $40 trillion debt — he says his own Chicago gas price rose from about $3.10 to $4.60 a gallon.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- He reviews Iran's reported 10-point ceasefire proposal (permanent ceasefire, reopened Hormuz with $2 million per-ship tolls shared with Oman, sanctions relief, right to enrich, war reparations) and argues every point amounts to validating Iran as the dominant power in the Persian Gulf — power that 300 years of history suggest no state voluntarily surrenders.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- His proposed off-ramp is an enforceable "military containment of Israel": a congressional bill cutting all US military and economic funds to Israel if it attacks Iran, paired with Israel joining the NPT and accepting IAEA monitoring as the quid pro quo for on-site inspection of Iran's enriched uranium — though he concedes Israel would likely spoil it.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
- Pape declares NATO "for all practical purposes dead," arguing no European military will again follow an American general's orders under Article 5 after this "catastrophic failure," and that helping secure Hormuz would be political suicide for European leaders — noting UK Prime Minister Starmer's favorability rose after refusing to send troops.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]

# Takeaways

Pape's closing assessment is that there is no way back to the pre-war status quo: within a year Iran could have nuclear weapons on the North Korea model — multiple bombs, sequential mountain tests — and the US faces a trap between a ground war it cannot win quickly and acquiescing to a new center of world power. He advises tracking troop movements, not rhetoric, as the true indicator of what comes next, argues the only real solution is a deal anchored in enforceable containment of Israel, and extends his escalation-trap logic to domestic politics, urging publics to escape the "legitimacy shock cycle" by voting for centrists rather than bouncing between extremes. Both host and guest close by centering the 92 million ordinary Iranians living under bombardment and threat.[^the-iran-war-expert-the-most-dangerous-stage-beg-pDKlaJM_vys]
