# Overview

Steven Bartlett convenes three entrepreneurs he calls "the avengers of entrepreneurship on the internet" — Daniel Priestley, Codie Sanchez and Alex Hormozi — to work through how someone with little or no capital would build a business today.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y] Their shared premise is that money is the least important input: Priestley argues the real assets are knowledge, network and reputation, and that cash is "a bit of a red herring."[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y] Sanchez frames the beginner's choice as two paths — partner with or work for the best operator you can reach (lower risk, mid-size returns), or go it alone (highest risk, highest reward) — and says neither is objectively better, only better for a given person.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]

Much of the conversation is about who you sell to and at what price. All three contend that most beginners undercharge because they "sell out of their own wallet," and that the biggest available gain is usually moving the same skill to a market where it produces far more value.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y] Hormozi supplies benchmarks (roughly seven of ten prospects should be saying no), Sanchez supplies case studies (a home-inspection business rescued by rebranding as "luxury home inspections"), and Priestley supplies segmentation frameworks (the "affluent niche" of the 9% who buy on passion rather than price or pedigree).[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]

The second half turns to content, influence and closing. The guests argue that content is now the cheapest way to build distribution, but only converts when it carries proof; Hormozi offers a four-part model of influence (status, power, credibility, likeness) to explain why small educational audiences out-earn enormous entertainment ones.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y] The episode closes with a game in which each guest is handed $1,000, $10,000 or $100,000 and asked how they would turn it into a scalable business — and all three answers converge on borrowing someone else's existing assets rather than spending the cash.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]

# Key points

- Priestley's MOAT test for whether a business is worth building: Margin (a good business has at least 15% net margin), Operations (does it scale or is it just a job), Advantage (an unfair edge, since "all arbitrage windows close"), and TAM. Score each 1–10; above 30 is "fund it," 20–30 is "fix it," below 20 is "flee it."[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Hormozi's "pain, passion, profession" filter for first businesses, with the easiest on-ramp being going from employed to self-employed doing work someone already pays you for — you already have delivery, so you only need to learn promotion and selling.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Sell where your skill creates the most value, not to whoever is nearest: Hormozi notes a conversion-rate firm adding 10% to a $1M business creates $100,000, while the same work on a $100M business creates $10M. Priestley cites a workplace-safety consultant who went from £2,000 to £20,000 a day (and £400,000 engagements) purely by repositioning into high-hazard manufacturing, and Bartlett describes earning "many millions" in biotech for the same social-media skills that earned tens of thousands in fashion.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Pricing diagnostics from Hormozi: you are roughly correctly priced when about 30–35% of prospects say yes; an 80% close rate implies a 2–3x price increase is sitting there, 60% implies 1.5–2x, and 20% means you need to learn to sell. He recounts tripling gym prices, losing a third of members, doubling revenue and cutting costs by two-thirds.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Sanchez's "luxury home inspections" example: adding one word to a failing home-inspection company's name and ads raised margins 45% and saved a business that was 45 days from running out of cash — no more volume, staff or skill required.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Priestley's customer pyramid: 1% of buyers hold 15% of the budget and shop on pedigree, 9% hold 45% and shop on passion, and 90% hold 40% and shop on price — so the "affluent niche" 9% is the best starting segment, and roughly 60% of disposable income sits with the top 10% of earners.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- On passive income, Hormozi argues it is a continuum rather than a binary and that beginners should buy "leverage on their active income" — a snow blower, or a phlebotomy certificate that doubles hourly earnings in weeks. Priestley reframes it as asset income and distinguishes traditional assets (art, wine, watches) from "performance assets" — intellectual property, media, code and data — that you build rather than buy. Sanchez calls idealised passive income partly a tax-code artefact and partly an industry narrative that moved assets to the wealthy class.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Hormozi's from-zero playbook, which he says he ran three times and used to make $100,000 in a month: learn to advertise, then pre-negotiate a wholesale price with an existing business (a gym, a chiropractor) and keep the spread, since there is no cost of delivery.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Hormozi's SPCL model of influence — status (control of scarce resources), power (say-do correspondence, as with Martha Stewart's recipes), credibility (proof) and likeness — explains why some creators with 50m+ followers have had repeated failed launches: views without influence. He argues "proof always beats promise" and recommends taking 10 clients free purely to generate proof.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Sanchez's contrast of expertise versus experience content (attention with intent to buy versus attention alone), illustrated with Rihanna, a billionaire through Fenty Beauty, versus Drake, who she says has arguably more attention but a fraction of the net worth because he built less intent to buy.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Sales mechanics: Hormozi's CLOSER framework (clarify, label, overview past pain, sell the vacation in three points, explain concerns, reinforce the decision), the finding from meta-analyses that salespeople who talk less close more, and the claim that waiting eight seconds after asking for the sale closes 30% more. Sanchez cites studies from Harvard, Stanford and Oxford or Cambridge in which women who wore makeup earned 20–40% more, and men in suits 15–18% more, with no attractiveness differential.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Both Hormozi and Priestley argue for adding "good friction" — assessments, applications, waiting lists — because it raises cost per lead but lifts show rates, close rates and cash collected; Priestley pitches the assessment rather than the service as one of his best closes, and Hormozi says a simple video sales letter can lift sales 20–40%.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Fundraising frameworks: Sanchez's "MIGHTIEST touch" reduces to profit, growth, history or — if you have none of those — a story, which is what Bartlett says he had at 18 when he gave a co-founder 30% equity in future money instead of salary. Priestley's pitch frameworks are name-same-fame-pain-aim-game for social settings and CAPSTONE for scheduled pitches.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- The suitcase game: Hormozi would pocket $1,000 and sell AI email-list reactivation to small businesses on pure revenue share; Sanchez would use $10,000 to source acquisition targets for local private equity firms for roughly $10,000 a deal while learning dealmaking; Priestley would put $100,000 into Sanchez's business as debt-for-equity plus sweat equity for 20% total, buying access to her network and reputation. Sanchez notes this is effectively the franchise model, and that 90% of startups fail.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
- Most undervalued games, per each guest: brand and distribution (Hormozi), financial engineering and access to other people's money via SBA loans and credit lines (Sanchez), the banana lesson that profit requires constrained supply and manufactured demand (Priestley), and exceptional hires (Bartlett). Hormozi also explains the thesis of his book *$100M Money Models* — that 30-day gross profit per customer should exceed twice CAC plus COGS, so customers finance the next customer's acquisition.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]

# Takeaways

Across three quite different operating styles, the guests keep returning to the same claim: the constraint on a new business is rarely money and almost always positioning — who you sell to, what proof you can show them, what you dare to charge, and whether you own any distribution.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y] They present pricing, friction and proof as the cheapest available levers, and treat borrowed leverage — someone else's customers, network, deal flow or brand — as the fastest substitute for capital you do not have.[^the-simple-proven-way-to-earn-100-000-from-nothi-hVlAOIUA71Y]
