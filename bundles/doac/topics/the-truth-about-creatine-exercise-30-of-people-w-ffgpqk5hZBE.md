# Overview

Dr Vonda Wright, an orthopaedic surgeon and longevity researcher who has spent three decades working with elite athletes, tells Steven Bartlett she is "on a rampage to make bones sexy again."[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] Her central claim is that most people treat the skeleton as static scaffolding when it is in fact an endocrine organ and a mineral storehouse — one whose signalling proteins reach the brain, pancreas, muscle and testes.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] She describes bone, muscle, tendon, ligament, cartilage and fat as a single ecosystem descended from the same mesenchymal stem cell, "not distant neighbours" but "cousins" speaking the same language.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]

Much of the conversation concerns timing. Wright argues for a "critical decade" from roughly 35 to 45, when people should get baseline labs (including testosterone), start heavy resistance training while hormones are still high, and build VO2 max before the roughly 10%-per-decade decline sets in.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] She is critical of health systems that will not fund a DEXA scan until 65 — "by 65 the damage is done" — and of clinicians who deliver a pre-diabetes diagnosis too casually, given that she says roughly 96 million Americans have it and that Alzheimer's is increasingly thought of as a third phase of diabetes.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]

The episode also covers her published work naming the "musculoskeletal syndrome of menopause," her practical prescriptions for lifting, protein and creatine, and a live demonstration of the Trendelenburg test on Bartlett to expose the glute imbalance behind his recurring running injuries.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] Throughout, she argues that the obstacle is not knowledge but motivation, and that people sustain habits only when the payoff is felt today rather than promised in twenty years.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]

# Key points

- Wright's headline fracture statistics: one in two women will have an osteoporotic fracture in her lifetime, women sustain 70% of hip fractures, 50% of people who break a hip never return to pre-fall function, and 30% die from the fracture's complications — bed rest, infections, the sequelae of being sedentary.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- She describes bone as a master communicator: osteocalcin released by osteoblasts travels to the brain (reducing inflammation and stimulating hippocampal neurogenesis via BDNF), to the pancreas (insulin sensitivity), to muscle (glucose uptake) and, in men, to the Leydig cells of the testes to produce testosterone; muscle answers back with irisin.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- On the bone–brain link, she says people with low bone density show higher rates of cognitive decline, and that roughly 30% of people with brain disease also have osteoporosis — a correlation she is careful to say has not been shown to be causal.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Her 2004 study of Masters athletes at the National Senior Games found chronic exercise maintains high bone density into the eighties, and that "bounding" impact sports (running, basketball, volleyball) matter as much to bone density as uncontrollable factors like age, sex chromosomes and family history — swimming and cycling do not deliver the same stimulus.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Three points in life where bone is lost, in her account: failing to build enough in youth (she cites University of Wisconsin research finding gymnastics builds the best bone, and blames sedentary childhoods plus the "teeny tiny" body ideal that disrupts menstrual cycles); breastfeeding, where women lose about 20% of bone density in the first six months and must replace roughly 500mg of calcium daily; and perimenopause from around 45, when 15% of bone density can go as oestrogen fails to restrain the osteoclasts.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- On diagnostics, she explains DEXA T-scores — 0 to −1 acceptable, −1 osteopenia, −2.5 osteoporosis, which she says raises fracture risk by 40% or more — and issues a public service announcement that anyone who has had a fracture should get a scan, since past fracture is the single best predictor of future fracture.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Her VO2 max argument: elite athletes reach 75–90 and "mere mortals" are excellent at around 50, but VO2 max declines about 10% per decade after midlife, and she never wants to reach the fragility line of 18 for men or 16 for women, the level at which people cannot rise from a chair unaided.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Her own regimen and prescriptions: lifting heavy four times a week at four reps and four sets (she suggests roughly eight reps, four sets for men in midlife), zone-two base training on other days, two sprint sessions, 130g of protein a day at one gram per ideal pound with at least 30g per meal, blueberries as the only fruit, and complex carbohydrates only. She notes she added about 50g of morning carbohydrate after her CGM showed she could not function with patients on protein alone.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- On creatine, she recounts using very high cycled doses with wrestlers at the 1992 Olympics and says the current evidence supports a steady 5g a day for men and women, helping build both muscle and brain — noting that when she asked her own office, only the men raised their hands, because women still believe it is "for bodybuilders."[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- She insists it is never too late: "your body will always rise to the strategic stress you place on it," citing a three-month programme that took people from 51% body fat and an inability to hold a plank to a 5K, and noting that a ten-week break from lifting preserves muscle memory.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Muscle as an endocrine organ: contraction transcribes klotho, the "longevity protein" first described in Nature about 30 years ago, and her own study found circulating klotho highest in young athletes, second highest in Masters athletes aged 70–80, and lowest in young sedentary people — older athletes beat sedentary youth.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- She and her group named and published the "musculoskeletal syndrome of menopause" — arthralgia, frozen shoulder (called "50-year-old woman's shoulder" in Asian cultures), roughly 20% sarcopenia, bone loss and tendon problems, all traced to empty oestrogen receptors on musculoskeletal tissue, affecting about 80% of women. The open-access paper has been downloaded nearly 300,000 times against a typical 10,000 for top journals' best articles.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- On running injuries, she argues runners who only run get hurt because running is a single-leg sport requiring glute strength to stabilise the pelvis; she demonstrates the Trendelenburg single-leg squat on Bartlett, identifies a weaker left glute matching his repeated injury, and prescribes single-leg step-ups, lunges and hip hitches. Her own predictable Achilles and hip-flexor pain she traces to big-toe arthritis from a lifetime in high heels.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
- Her Masters athletics study of track and field found top finishers do not significantly slow until past 70 — the winning 70-year-old miler ran just under seven minutes against a 4:34 for the over-50 winner and 4:17 for the high-school champion — which she reads as evidence that earlier decline is psychology and training choices, not biology.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]

# Takeaways

Wright's argument is that the decline most people accept as ageing is largely the compound interest of choices made — or not made — in their thirties and forties, and that bone, muscle, metabolism and brain rise and fall together rather than separately.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] Her prescription is unglamorous and specific: impact and heavy resistance training, high protein, creatine, baseline labs early, a DEXA scan without waiting for insurance, and hormone decisions "based on science and not fear."[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE] She is candid that the binding constraint is behavioural, arguing that promises about life at 70 never move anyone and that habits stick only when they make you feel better tomorrow.[^the-truth-about-creatine-exercise-30-of-people-w-ffgpqk5hZBE]
