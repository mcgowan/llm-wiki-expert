# Overview

In this episode of The Diary Of A CEO, host Steven Bartlett speaks with biochemist Jessie Inchauspé, known online as the "Glucose Goddess," about her new book *Nine Months That Count Forever: How Your Pregnancy Diet Shapes Your Baby's Future*.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w] Inchauspé's central argument is that a pregnant mother is not merely a "bun in the oven" providing heat and time: what she eats during pregnancy places epigenetic "dimmer switches" on the baby's DNA that shape its brain development and its lifelong vulnerability to diabetes, obesity, and psychiatric disorders.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w] She stresses that this is not the mother's fault but the fault of a food system and marketing culture that keep parents uninformed, and she says she wrote the book after reading roughly 2,000 scientific papers when she became pregnant herself.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]

The first part of the conversation extends her earlier work on blood glucose: she argues that glucose spikes and crashes drive inflammation, faster aging, cravings, fatigue, mood instability, and even compulsive behaviors like doom scrolling, and that food marketing claims such as "no added sugar," "gluten-free," and "vegan" systematically mislead consumers — a glass of orange juice, she claims, contains the same roughly 25 grams of sugar as a glass of Coca-Cola, absorbed by the body in exactly the same way.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]

The second half focuses on four nutrients she says most pregnant women get wrong — choline, sugar/glucose, protein, and omega-3s — alongside guidance the guest offers on exercise, alcohol, caffeine, supplements, and continuous glucose monitoring. Inchauspé also speaks candidly about her own silent miscarriage, noting that one in five pregnancies ends in miscarriage, and about the anxiety that shadowed her subsequent pregnancy.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]

# Key points

- Inchauspé argues that maternal diet epigenetically programs the baby: mothers with very high glucose levels during pregnancy, she says, have babies whose DNA carries switches making them more vulnerable to diabetes, obesity, and psychiatric disorders later in life.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She claims 90% of mothers do not get enough choline during pregnancy — a nutrient that forms the baby's brain — and that only 6% of doctors discuss choline with pregnant patients; four eggs a day (about 450 mg of choline) is her recommended fix, at roughly $1 per day.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She cites a Cornell study in which mothers given double the recommended choline had babies with 10% faster reaction times on an infant test correlated with adult IQ.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- On sugar, she highlights the UK's 1940-1953 sugar ration: researchers who followed up with about 60,000 people found those gestated during the ration (roughly 40 g of maternal sugar per day versus 80 g afterward) had a 15% lower likelihood of developing type 2 diabetes in their lifetime.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- The hosts discuss studies linking maternal diabetes to child outcomes, including a Danish JAMA study showing a 15% higher risk of psychiatric disorders (with schizophrenia risk 55% higher) and a 2025 Lancet review of 200 studies covering 56 million mother-baby pairs showing a 25% higher autism risk — with Bartlett noting these are correlations, not proven causation.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- Inchauspé's proposed mechanism is inflammation making microglia overactive so they destroy healthy neurons during fetal brain formation, which occurs at a claimed rate of 250,000 neurons per minute.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She argues orange juice is nutritionally equivalent to Coca-Cola in sugar terms (~25 g per glass, the WHO's full daily limit) and says she hopes it disappears from school lunches and hospital meals; she calls "no added sugars" one of the most deceptive marketing labels.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- On protein, she says mothers need about 1.6 g per kilo of body weight per day in the third trimester, and that animal studies suggest protein-restricted pregnancies epigenetically program babies to stay smaller with less muscle throughout life.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She recommends omega-3s (especially DHA) via fatty fish two to three times a week plus supplementation, claiming about 75% of people are deficient, and says babies need no fructose at all during pregnancy — but do need about 70 g per day of glucose from starches in the late third trimester.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She describes a rat study in which pups of mothers who walked 30 minutes daily on treadmills during pregnancy solved mazes twice as fast and showed fewer anxiety symptoms, attributing the effect to elevated BDNF, and argues exercise in pregnancy is strongly beneficial.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- She advocates zero alcohol during pregnancy, citing a University of Melbourne 3D-imaging study finding facial changes and weaker brain connectivity even at low doses, while saying up to two cups of coffee a day appears neutral.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- Citing a Diabetes Care study of 700 women, she argues first-trimester continuous glucose monitoring can predict gestational diabetes at 24-28 weeks — evidence, she says, that gestational diabetes is not random but rooted in pre-pregnancy glucose levels.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
- Her everyday "glucose hacks" include a vegetable starter before meals, avoiding sugar on an empty stomach, a savory high-protein breakfast, and using muscles (walks, squats, calf raises engaging the soleus) within roughly 90 minutes of eating to blunt spikes.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]

# Takeaways

The guest's closing message is that pregnancy is a window of outsized influence: mothers have real agency over their baby's foundations, but they are being failed by a food system, deceptive marketing, and an information gap between decades of research and standard prenatal advice.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w] Her prescription — as she frames it, not medical consensus — is a simple, inexpensive four-nutrient plan (choline from eggs, minimal fructose, ample protein, omega-3s from fatty fish) plus movement, and she goes as far as saying she would outlaw health claims on food packaging and fruit juice in schools if she could change one thing about the world.[^they-re-lying-about-healthy-foods-sugar-shocking-Xm_PHZXGe-w]
