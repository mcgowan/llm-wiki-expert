# Overview

In this roundtable, Steven Bartlett — who states up front that he has no settled opinion on the subject — hosts two prominent voices on unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP): Dan Farah, producer-director of the documentary *The Age of Disclosure*, and Dr. Hal Puthoff, a quantum physicist who says he worked for the NSA and CIA, advised Bigelow Aerospace, and served as a senior scientist on classified US government UAP programs.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM] The guests' central claim is that elements of the US government have concealed the existence of non-human intelligent life since the late 1940s through a hidden "legacy program" — allegedly run by parts of the CIA, Air Force, Department of Energy, and major defense contractors, outside congressional and even presidential oversight — while racing China and Russia to reverse-engineer recovered technology of non-human origin.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]

The conversation is pegged to news events the guests describe: a February directive by President Trump instructing federal agencies to declassify UAP evidence, and the release days before recording of a first tranche of roughly 400 files, whose most notable item they say is a 1972 Apollo-mission still image of a triangular craft over the moon.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM] Farah recounts interviewing Secretary of State Marco Rubio, Navy pilots, generals, and intelligence leaders such as Jay Stratton, several of whom he says attest to crash retrievals and non-human bodies; Puthoff argues the observed flight characteristics are consistent with engineering Einstein's general relativity equations — warp bubbles and metric engineering — which humans understand theoretically but cannot build.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]

Bartlett repeatedly plays skeptic, asking why no clear iPhone footage exists, why NASA's study found no alien evidence, why Elon Musk denies encountering anything with 9,000 satellites, and whether eyewitness testimony can carry such extraordinary claims; the episode closes with a long detour into Puthoff's CIA-funded "Stargate" remote-viewing program.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]

# Key points

- Farah claims an 80-year cover-up of non-human intelligent life by elements of the US government since at least the late 1940s, sustained by a deliberately manufactured stigma he calls "the most effective disinformation campaign in the history of the US government."[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- The guests describe a deeply hidden "legacy program" — elements of the CIA, Air Force, Department of Energy, and defense contractors — which they say has operated outside oversight since the 1940s, undermined the AATIP investigation (started 2008, defunded 2010), and has been kept even from sitting presidents.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Farah says multiple on-the-record interviewees, including former UAP Task Force director Jay Stratton, attest to UAP crash retrievals with non-human bodies; sources allege at least four types of non-human life and dozens of craft recoveries in the US alone.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- The stated rationale for secrecy is "you can't tell your friends without telling your enemies" — a Cold War-rooted technology race with Russia and China to reverse-engineer non-human technology — which the guests argue has now flipped into a reason for disclosure, since stigma keeps US scientists out of the field.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- They report that Trump's February declassification directive produced a first tranche of files including the alleged 1972 Apollo triangle-craft photo, but claim most agencies engaged minimally and are "pushing back hard" against handing over evidence.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Both guests emphasize UAP activity over nuclear weapons sites — including claims that UAP have shut down US missiles and briefly initiated a launch countdown at a Soviet base — and say intelligence agencies learned to "bait" UAP by concentrating nuclear assets.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Puthoff argues the observed physics (instant acceleration to 80,000 feet in the 2004 Tic Tac incident, transmedium travel, hundreds of miles per hour underwater) matches solutions of general relativity — warp drives and wormholes — that are theoretically valid but beyond human engineering and materials science.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- To Bartlett's objection that ubiquitous smartphones should have produced clear footage, Farah answers that a craft's "warp bubble" distorts imagery like photographing koi through water, while claiming indisputable classified videos exist, including VHS footage of a triangle craft over a nuclear site.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- On NASA's contrary report, Farah alleges the study was designed to conclude "nothing to see here" and that panel member Mike Gold said the team was told to exclude the Apollo image; on Elon Musk's denials, the guests suggest unacknowledged special access programs legally require non-acknowledgment.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Farah cites whistleblower retaliation — David Grusch's career damage and a special forces source who withdrew saying he would be "forfeiting my life" — and says the White House is now assuring gatekeepers that disclosure is "not a witch hunt."[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Puthoff recounts his CIA-funded Stargate remote-viewing program from the 1970s: claims include viewers locating a downed Soviet plane in Africa within 3 miles (a story echoed in a Jimmy Carter recording played in the episode), a silver-futures experiment netting $260,000 in 30 days, and a psychic altering a magnetically shielded quantum device at Stanford.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
- Bartlett's stated position remains open-minded skepticism: other life in the universe is probabilistically near-certain, but he says he needs more than eyewitness testimony to accept that it has visited Earth — a stance the guests call reasonable pending further declassification, while predicting a president will eventually announce "we're not alone."[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]

# Takeaways

The episode is an extended airing of the disclosure movement's strongest claims, made by two of its most connected advocates, against a host who explicitly withholds endorsement.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM] The guests' closing argument is that UAP is "the most bipartisan issue in Washington," that a rolling declassification process is underway with more tranches expected within weeks, and that confirmed disclosure would trigger a technology boom and possibly unify humanity — but nearly every substantive claim in the conversation rests on the guests' accounts of classified information, off-the-record sources, and witness testimony rather than publicly verifiable evidence, a limitation Bartlett highlights throughout.[^ufo-roundtable-former-cia-scientist-proves-alien-V-z0k5xu1hM]
