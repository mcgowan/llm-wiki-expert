# Overview

In this episode, Steven Bartlett interviews Dr. Alan Goldhamer, a physician who has spent roughly 40 years practicing medically supervised water-only fasting at the TrueNorth Health Center and who co-authored the book "Can Fasting Save Your Life."[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0] Goldhamer's core argument is that most modern chronic disease — hypertension, type 2 diabetes, autoimmune conditions, and some cancers — stems from dietary excess in a world where 76% of people are overweight or obese, and where salt, oil, and sugar ("SOS") added to food fool the brain's satiety mechanisms by overstimulating dopamine, driving passive overeating.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0] He contends that the resulting visceral fat "acts like a tumor," giving off inflammatory products that cause seemingly unrelated diseases, and that fasting is the fastest way to undo that damage.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]

Goldhamer defines true fasting as the complete abstinence from all substances except purified water, undertaken in a state of complete rest, and his clinic supervises fasts of 5 to 40 days with laboratory monitoring and a carefully staged refeeding protocol lasting about half the length of the fast.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0] He walks through the physiology: after roughly 24 hours glycogen stores are depleted and the brain switches from glucose to ketones (chiefly beta-hydroxybutyric acid), an adaptation he says allows a 70-kg man to survive about 70 days without food and which raises BDNF, triggers autophagy, mobilizes visceral fat, reboots the gut microbiome, and produces many of the same cardiometabolic changes as exercise.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]

The guest presents his published outcome data — most prominently on hypertension and lymphoma case reports — while stressing that fasting manages rather than cures disease: benefits persist only if patients adopt a whole-plant-food, SOS-free diet afterward.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0] His practical prescription is that everyone should fast 12 hours daily, that even healthy people should consider a supervised week-long fast once a year, and that longer therapeutic fasts belong in a controlled, monitored setting rather than being attempted alone at home.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]

# Key points

- Goldhamer reports a study with T. Colin Campbell in which 174 consecutive hypertensive patients all normalized their blood pressure without medication after supervised water-only fasting, and calls fasting the most effective treatment ever shown for high blood pressure, the leading cause of death and disability.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- In a recent prospective study with a Mayo Clinic colleague, he says 26 of 27 hypertensive patients who completed fasting achieved normal blood pressure without medication (the remaining one halved their dose), and 76% had maintained their weight loss at one-year follow-up; patients starting with stage 3 hypertension lost an average of 60 points of systolic pressure.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- He explains that after about 24 hours of fasting glycogen is depleted and the brain converts to burning ketones (beta-hydroxybutyric acid) from fat, an evolutionary adaptation that he says lets a 70-kg male fast up to about 70 days before starvation — though his clinic caps fasts at 40 days, a length only about 1% of patients undergo.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- Citing DEXA body-composition studies, he claims a two-week fast typically costs 10% of body weight, 20% of total fat, and 40% of visceral fat but only 6% of lean tissue, with lean mass fully recovered — and proportionally higher than baseline — by six weeks while fat loss continues during refeeding.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- The guest argues that salt, oil, and sugar are not food but hyper-concentrated chemicals that hijack dopamine-driven satiety signals, so that, for example, salted rice or bread (1,500 calories a pound) drives "passive overeating" in a way unseasoned whole foods like matzah do not; he notes 70% of the average person's calories now come from ultra-processed foods.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- He describes rodent research in which periodic fasting or feeding at 60% of ad libitum intake extends lifespan 30-100%, but reframes the finding: fasting doesn't double lifespan, overfeeding cuts it in half.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- Goldhamer recounts a BMJ case report of a woman with progressing follicular lymphoma whose tumors regressed during a 21-day water fast and who remained cancer-free on CT at 3 years and again at 10 years on a whole-plant-food SOS-free diet, plus a published case series including a stage 4 patient who had failed chemotherapy and improved after 3-week and 39-day fasts — while cautioning this is remission and management, not cure.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- Fasting, he says, "reboots" the gut microbiome, and the host cites a 2024 study finding harmful fusobacteria dropped by more than 80% after a 7-day water-only fast; Goldhamer links this reboot to improvements in ulcerative colitis, Crohn's, IBS, depression, and anxiety, noting most of the body's serotonin and dopamine is produced in the gut.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- He argues women's hormone-linked conditions — PCOS, fibrocystic breast disease, dysmenorrhea, menorrhagia — respond consistently to fasting because the gut microbiome and liver, both profoundly affected by fasting, govern the breakdown of estradiol to estriol; some previously infertile patients conceived within a cycle or two after fasting.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- Safety, in his telling, hinges on candidate screening (history, exam, kidney and liver labs), complete rest to avoid gluconeogenesis breaking down lean tissue, twice-daily monitoring for orthostatic hypotension and electrolyte shifts (fasts are terminated if potassium falls below 3.0), and progressive refeeding — about one day of 600-calorie fruit and vegetable juices per week fasted — to avoid potentially fatal refeeding syndrome.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- He claims fasting detoxifies: fat biopsies show accumulated PCBs, dioxins, pesticides, and heavy metals — as much as 90% of some toxins coming from animal foods that bioconcentrate pollution — and fasting rapidly mobilizes and eliminates them, an effect he says juice fasting and fasting-mimicking diets do not match.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
- Extreme cases and future work: a dentist with 16 years of constant daily head pain after a traumatic brain injury resolved her headaches after a 41-day fast and a second 40-day fast, remaining headache-free for 12 years; Goldhamer is now launching a lifelong study of 2,000-3,000 people aiming to prevent the average 16 years of end-of-life debility through diet, sleep, exercise, and periodic fasting.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]

# Takeaways

Goldhamer's closing message is that health comes from healthful living — diet, sleep, and exercise — not from "pills, potions, and powders," and that fasting works only as a reset and motivator for permanent lifestyle change, since conditions like hypertension and lymphoma return if patients resume the diets that caused them.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0] He advises everyone to fast 12 hours daily (stopping eating 3-4 hours before bed), suggests healthy people consider an annual supervised week-long fast, and insists longer water fasts be done only after medical screening and under supervision; he also observes that only about 2-2.5% of people objectively measure as healthy, and that overcoming a supposedly hopeless diagnosis transforms patients' entire paradigm of what is possible.[^world-no-1-fasting-expert-the-link-between-cance-jDG1m_b5Ih0]
