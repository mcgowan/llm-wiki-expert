# Overview

Historian Yuval Noah Harari tells Steven Bartlett that his mission across his books is to clarify the global conversation, and that the central warning of that work is that much of what people take to be real is fiction. On his account humans rule the planet not through individual genius but through flexible large-scale cooperation, which is made possible by shared stories, and money, corporations, nations and religions are all such stories; the danger is that the tools stop serving us and start running us.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] He applies the same argument to war, insisting that the conflict he has just come home to in Israel, like most wars in history, is not really about territory or food but about incompatible mythologies.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]

On AI, Harari says the technology is moving faster than he expected when he wrote *Homo Deus* in 2016 and that the world is not ready.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] He objects to comparisons with the printing press or the industrial revolution on two grounds: those tools could not make decisions or generate ideas of their own, so they ultimately empowered humanity, while AI can take power away from it; and humanity's adaptation to the industrial revolution, which he grades a C minus, ran through European imperialism, Soviet communism and Nazism as failed experiments in what an industrial society should look like.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] A second round of such experiments, this time with AI and bioengineering, would in his view finish the species.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]

The conversation's second half turns to war, attention and the mind. Harari argues the relatively peaceful early twenty-first century was the product of deliberate decisions, not a miracle, and that the liberal order underpinning it has been attacked from outside by Russia, North Korea and Iran and from inside by nationalist politics, with no alternative offered in its place.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] He describes the platform economy as having hacked human attention and warns that intimacy is the next target, then offers his own countermeasures: two hours of daily meditation, an annual retreat, and a defence of boredom as the neighbour of peace.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]

# Key points

- Harari argues AI differs in kind from every previous technology because it is the first that can make decisions and create new ideas by itself, which is why it can take power away from humans rather than amplify their reach.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He grades humanity's adaptation to the industrial revolution a C minus, citing European imperialism, Soviet communism and Nazism as the failed experiments in building an industrial society, and argues a repeat performance with AI would end the species.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He expects homo sapiens to end not in nuclear catastrophe but through self-modification via bioengineering, AI and brain-computer interfaces, becoming as different from us as we are from chimpanzees or Neanderthals, though on Neuralink specifically he says he is still waiting for evidence, since we are far from understanding the brain and further from understanding the mind.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He warns that finance is where regulation is absent: the 2007-08 crisis began with CDOs almost nobody understood, and he sees no effort to keep the financial system at a complexity humans can grasp, unlike the attention paid to AI-designed pathogens.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- His scenario for algorithmic government is not voting for an AI but human figureheads: a 4am call telling the prime minister a meltdown is 30 minutes away, with three options and no time to explain how the algorithm chose one.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He calls belief in free will an incuriosity that closes off self-investigation, argues cultural and biological factors shape most decisions, and says people with a mystical belief in free will are the easiest to manipulate because they do not think they can be; humans, he says, are now hackable animals.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- Algorithms already decide loans, mortgages, university places, job applications, prison sentences and, through dating apps, romantic life, often with no explanation available when a human asks why.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He separates intelligence from consciousness, noting computers already exceed humans in narrow fields with zero consciousness, and argues a relationship with an entity that mimics feelings without having them is both a manipulation risk and a question about the health of the human psyche.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- On longevity he argues the goal is a-mortality rather than immortality, and that people who can live indefinitely but still die in accidents would live in extreme anxiety; he also asks why anyone deferring their life to a future cure does not simply live that life now.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He opposes genetic and cognitive enhancement on the grounds it would translate economic inequality into biological inequality, and that many supposed upgrades will prove downgrades, with governments and armies boosting intelligence and discipline while disregarding compassion; unlike AI, he argues, a bioengineering arms race is one a cautious society wins.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- On universal basic income he asks whether it is universal or merely national, pointing out that taxing Californian tech firms to retrain workers in Ohio does nothing for Guatemala or Pakistan once robots make shirts more cheaply than they do.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
- He says the era of peace is over and more wars are coming unless order is rebuilt, notes defence budgets rising in a vicious circle, and argues the patriotism-versus-globalism choice is a false binary since global cooperation means shared norms, not world government.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]

# Takeaways

Harari's practical response is personal before it is political: a balanced information diet, two hours of meditation daily and a 30-to-60-day annual retreat with no phones, emails or books, on the argument that the mind deserves the attention people give their bodies and that constant immersion in the news cycle destroys perspective.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] He identifies excitement as what is wrong with the world, noting that fear, hate and anger are all forms of excitement, that an organism excited all the time dies, and that peace and boredom are neighbours in the way outrage and excitement are.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM] His closing note is that humans still have agency because most power remains in human hands, and his advice is to pick the single issue closest to your heart and pursue it through cooperation, since fifty people in an organisation achieve more than five hundred isolated activists.[^yuval-noah-harari-an-urgent-warning-they-hope-yo-UzOJiqN_DpM]
